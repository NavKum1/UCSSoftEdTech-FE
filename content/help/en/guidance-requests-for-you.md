People who aren't sure which area or certification to choose can ask Edulexa for guidance. When Edulexa's advisors are busy, they hand some requests to approved community mentors. You get a notification and an e-mail for each one.

## Your requests

Open [Guidance requests for you](/mentor/guidance). Each request shows:

- the person's name and where the request is, such as [ui: With a mentor] or [ui: Answered];
- [ui: What they're unsure about:] and their answers to Edulexa's questions;
- their counseling quiz result, if they chose to share it;
- the conversation so far.

Files they attached are shown to the advisors only. A request leaves your list once it's resolved or handed back.

## Reply

Write your reply and select [ui: Send]. The person gets it in Edulexa and by e-mail, and the request shows [ui: Answered].

You can also reply to the e-mail you received about the request, when it says you can. Replies are taken only from the e-mail address on your mentor profile.

To leave a message only the advisors can read, tick [ui: Note for staff only (the person doesn't see it)], then select [ui: Add note].

## Hand it back

If you can't help, select [ui: Hand back to the advisors]. The request goes back to them straight away, and they're told by e-mail. Add a note for staff first if it helps them, for example who could help instead.
