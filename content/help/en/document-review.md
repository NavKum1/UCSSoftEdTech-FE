A career counselor reads your document and sends back a summary, a checklist and comments. Open [Document review](/student/document-review).

## Ask for a review

1. Select [ui: Request a review].
2. Choose the [ui: Document type]: a résumé, cover letter, personal statement, LinkedIn summary or something else.
3. Under [ui: What it's for], say what you're applying for, for example "Software internships".
4. Choose how to share it: [ui: Upload a file] (PDF, Word, PNG, JPEG or text, up to 10 MB), [ui: Paste text], or, for a résumé, [ui: From your library].
5. If you like, paste the job or program description, add notes for the reviewer, and give your deadline. A close deadline gets priority.
6. Select [ui: Send for review].

The page says when a counselor aims to return it. A résumé file you upload here is also saved in your [résumé library](/help/resumes).

[shot: document-review-request]

## Read the review

You get a notification when it's ready. Open it from [ui: My reviews]. Each version shows:

- the [ui: Summary],
- the [ui: Checklist], with each point rated (for example Strong or Needs work),
- the [ui: Comments]. On pasted text, each comment points to the passage it's about.

The reviewer may also attach a marked-up copy of your file to download.

If the counselor approves a résumé you sent from your library, it shows [ui: Approved by your school] there. Some schools take only approved résumés with job applications.

## Next steps

- Select [ui: Send a new version] to have your changes reviewed. Earlier versions and their comments stay on the page.
- Select [ui: Mark as done] when you're finished.
- Tell the career center how it went under [ui: Was this review helpful?], then select [ui: Save rating].
