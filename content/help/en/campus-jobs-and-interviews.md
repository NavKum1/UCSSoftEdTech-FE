## Find a job

The [Job board](/student/jobs) lists the jobs and internships employers offer through your school (campus drives) that your school has approved. Each one is checked against your academic record: [ui: Eligible], [ui: Not eligible] with the reasons, or [ui: Data missing]. If your record is wrong, ask your school to correct it.

Select [ui: Details and apply] to see the description, who can apply, and the rounds.

## Apply

1. Attach the documents it asks for: tick files you uploaded before, a résumé [ui: From your library], or choose a [ui: Document type] and upload a new file.
2. If the employer is named, tick the box to agree to share your details and documents with it.
3. Select [ui: Apply] (some say [ui: Opt in]).

Follow it under [My applications](/student/jobs/applications): each round's result, your interview times and any offer. While it's [ui: Applied] or [ui: In selection], you can select [ui: Withdraw application]. That stops sharing your details with the employer.

## If you get an offer

Select [ui: Accept] or [ui: Decline] before the date shown. If you already accepted another offer, [ui: Accept and release my earlier offer] declines the earlier one. Some offers come with a letter to sign: type your full name and select [ui: Sign and accept].
## Sign up for on-campus interviews

[Interviews](/student/interviews) lists interviews employers hold at your school, for students who meet their requirements or are invited.

1. Under [ui: Find interviews], select [ui: Sign up].
2. Choose an [ui: Interview time]. Times are in your school's time zone.
3. The first time with an employer, choose a résumé to share and agree to share your details.
4. Select [ui: Book this time].

No time left? Select [ui: Join alternate list]: you're booked automatically if a time opens up. Your bookings are under [ui: My interviews], where you can [ui: Cancel interview] until the time shown.

> If you miss too many interviews, new sign-ups are paused until the career center clears it.
