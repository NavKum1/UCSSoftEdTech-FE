An assessment must be published before you can give it to a class. See [Create an assessment](/help/create-an-assessment).

1. On the assessment, find [ui: Give it to] and [ui: Choose a class].
2. Leave [ui: Opens (optional)] empty to open it now, or pick a date and time.
3. Set when it's [ui: Due].
4. Decide whether to [ui: Accept late work (marked late)].
5. Select [ui: Give to class].

Students are e-mailed when you give it to the class, and reminded before the due date. They find it under [ui: My assessments].

You can give the same assessment to several classes, each with its own dates. Each class then has its own page of students and their work.

## Following the class

The class's page shows every student and where they are: [ui: Not started], [ui: Working on it], [ui: Viva next], [ui: Ready for review] and [ui: Decided]. Open a student's evidence from there, or use the [ui: Review] page for everything waiting across your classes.

> To withdraw it from a class before anyone has started, use [ui: Take back].
