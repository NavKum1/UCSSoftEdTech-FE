Appointments are for counselors and career center admins. Open [ui: Appointments] from the [ui: Students] menu.

## Set when students can book you

1. Open the [ui: Availability] tab.
2. In [ui: My booking profile], fill in the [ui: Name students see], your [ui: Video meeting link] and your [ui: In-person location]. Tick the [ui: Appointment types offered] you take, and tick [ui: Students can book me]. Select [ui: Save profile].
3. Under [ui: Weekly hours], select [ui: Add hours] for each block of time, then [ui: Save weekly hours]. Students can't book you until you add hours.
4. For a day off or a one-off extra block, use [ui: Time off and extra hours].

Times are in your school's time zone. Career admins can pick any counselor at the top of the tab and change their hours. Admins also create the kinds of appointments students can book, under [ui: Appointment types].

## Your schedule

Students book an open time themselves, so there's nothing to accept. The [ui: Schedule] tab shows what's booked, for one day or seven days, for you or for all counselors. To book on a student's behalf, select [ui: Book for a student], find the student, choose a type and a time, and select [ui: Book appointment]. The student is told.

## After the meeting

Open an appointment to:

- Record [ui: Attended], [ui: No-show] or [ui: Late cancellation], once it has started.
- Add [ui: Advising notes]. Choose [ui: Career staff only] or [ui: Also the student]. Notes are never shared with employers.
- Add [ui: Follow-up tasks] for the student or for you, with a due date.
- [ui: Cancel appointment], with a reason the student receives.

## See bookings in your own calendar

Copy the link under [ui: Private calendar feed] into Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar. Keep it private: anyone with it can see students' names. Or connect your work calendar in [ui: Calendar connection]. Depending on your school's choices, busy times in it aren't offered to students, and your bookings are added to it.
