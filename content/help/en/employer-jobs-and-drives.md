A job you send to a school becomes a campus drive at that school. Students see it only after the school's career center approves it.

## Before you post

Open [ui: Campus jobs] under [ui: Hiring]. [ui: Company verification] must show [ui: Verified] before you can post.

- A company that Edulexa staff verified is ready.
- A company in India can verify itself with its GSTIN, signed in with a company e-mail address.
- Other companies need Edulexa staff to verify them. The account owner selects [ui: Ask Edulexa to verify us] under [ui: Company verification]. The page then shows the date you asked, and you're told in the app and by e-mail when staff decide. See [Set up your company](/help/employer-getting-started).

## Post a job

1. Under [ui: New job or internship], fill in the [ui: Title], the [ui: Type], the [ui: Currency] and the pay.
2. Add the [ui: Locations], [ui: Degrees], [ui: Programs], [ui: Graduation years], the lowest grade you accept, and your [ui: Selection rounds] in order.
3. Set the [ui: Application deadline].
4. Under [ui: Who receives it], choose [ui: Schools I choose], or [ui: All schools that approved my company (each still approves)].
5. Under [ui: How students respond], choose [ui: Apply with documents] and tick the [ui: Documents students must attach], or choose [ui: Opt in].
6. Select [ui: Save as draft].

Open the job from [ui: Your jobs] and select [ui: Publish].

## Send it to schools

On the job's page, under [ui: Send to schools], tick the schools. Add a [ui: Drive date] and a [ui: Mode] if you know them, then select the send button.

[ui: Drives at schools] shows each school's drive, its status, the number of applications and the school's note. Some schools approve drives from verified employers automatically; those show [ui: Approved automatically].

- [ui: Pause] stops schools taking applications. Select [ui: Publish] to start again.
- [ui: Close job] closes it for good.
- [ui: Withdraw] takes the drive back from one school.

## Follow a drive

Select a school's name to open its drive. The top shows the deadline, whether it's taking applications, and the school's rules, such as one offer per student.

Applicants appear once the school approves the drive. You see the details students chose to share with your company when they applied. Each time the list is shown, it's recorded in the school's log of who saw student information. From the drive you can:

- add the selection rounds with [ui: Add round];
- shortlist applicants, and select [ui: Record result for selected] after each round;
- set [ui: Interview slots] for the shortlist;
- [ui: Make an offer] to an applicant, who can accept or decline it, and issue [ui: Offer letters] for students to sign.

## Career fairs

[ui: Career fairs] lists upcoming fairs at the schools that approved you. Each fair links to the school's own event page, where you request a booth.
