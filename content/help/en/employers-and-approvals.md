Employers create a company account in Edulexa and ask to recruit at your school. Career admins and employer relations staff decide. Your home page shows how many requests are waiting.

## Approve an employer

1. Open [ui: Employers] from the [ui: Employers and jobs] menu.
2. The [ui: Pending] tab lists new requests, with the company's details, its website and its note to you. A badge shows whether Edulexa could confirm the company. [ui: Staff verified] means Edulexa staff checked the company when it asked them to. The badge is there to help you decide; it doesn't approve anyone.
3. Write a [ui: Note to the employer (optional)] if you like, then select [ui: Approve], [ui: Decline] or [ui: Block]. A blocked company can't ask again.

The employer is told either way. You can change a decision later from the [ui: Approved], [ui: Declined] or [ui: Blocked] tab.

Once approved, an employer can send your school jobs and interview schedules and, if your school has one, browse the [résumé book](/help/resume-book).

## Approve their jobs

Each job an employer sends your school arrives as a campus drive and waits for you in [ui: Campus drives], on the [ui: Approvals] tab. Only approved drives are shown to students.

1. Open a drive to see the job, the pay, the deadline and whether the employer is verified.
2. Check the eligibility rules for your school, such as programs and graduation years.
3. Select [ui: Approve], or [ui: Don't approve] with a [ui: Note to the employer] saying why.

When you approve, eligible students are told.

## Your school's rules

On the [ui: Rules] tab, a career admin can approve drives from verified employers automatically and set placement rules, such as one offer per student.

Counselors can see campus drives but don't decide on them or on employers.
