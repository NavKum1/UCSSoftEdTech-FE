## Ask Edulexa

Not sure which area, course or certification is right for you? Ask a person.

1. Open [ui: Counseling quiz] from the [ui: Guidance] menu. You don't need to take the quiz first.
2. Select [ui: Ask Edulexa].
3. Fill in the short form. If you've taken the quiz, you can tick [ui: Also share my latest quiz answers and result (optional).]
4. Send it.

Edulexa's advisors read every request. Some are passed to a community mentor who knows the area. Nothing goes to an institute unless you approve it.

Prefer to ask a particular institute? Select [ui: Talk to a specific institute], then the institute's button to ask them. An advisor there contacts you by e-mail.

## Follow the replies

[ui: My guidance requests] lists each question you've asked, with its replies and where it stands.

- Write back in the box under a request and select [ui: Send], or reply to our e-mail. Both end up in the same place.
- If an advisor thinks an institute could help, the request says so. Select [ui: Yes, send it] to send them your request, or [ui: No, thanks].
- To ask about something else, select [ui: Ask something new].

## Find a mentor

[ui: Mentoring] in the [ui: Guidance] menu is free mentoring from people in the Edulexa community. The Edulexa team reviews every mentor before you can see them.

1. Mentors and mentees reach each other by e-mail, so your account needs a verified e-mail address first.
2. Select [ui: Join the community], then [ui: Open mentoring].
3. Say what you'd like help with, see the mentors who match and why, and ask one. A mentor accepts before you share contact details.

How it works once you're matched is in [Find a mentor](/help/mentoring).

### Become a mentor

Have experience to share? Select [ui: Apply to mentor] and fill in your mentor profile. You choose your topics, how you meet and how many mentees you take. The Edulexa team reviews your profile before members can see it.
