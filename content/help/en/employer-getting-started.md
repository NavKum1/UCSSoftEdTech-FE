To recruit at a school through Edulexa, your company needs an employer account, and the school's career center must approve it.

## Create your company account

1. Open the employer area. If your company isn't set up yet, you see [ui: Set up your company].
2. Fill in your company's details and select [ui: Create company account]. You're the account owner.

If a colleague already set up your company, ask them to invite you instead.

Your home page shows how Edulexa checked your company:

- [ui: E-mail address checked]: your sign-in e-mail address ends with your company website's name, so Edulexa confirmed the company automatically. A free e-mail service doesn't count.
- [ui: Unverified]: Edulexa couldn't confirm the company automatically. Schools may ask for more detail before they approve you.
- [ui: Staff verified]: Edulexa staff checked and verified your company.
- [ui: Under review]: you can't ask new schools until the review is finished.

## Ask Edulexa to verify your company

To post campus jobs, your company must be verified for campus hiring. Edulexa staff do this. A company in India can also verify itself with its GSTIN on the [ui: Campus jobs] page.

1. The account owner selects [ui: Ask Edulexa to verify us] on the home page, under [ui: Verification], or on the [ui: Campus jobs] page. You don't need to write to support.
2. The page shows the date you asked. Asking again while you wait doesn't send a second request.
3. Edulexa staff check your company. You're told in the app and by e-mail when they decide.

If Edulexa staff don't verify your company, the home page shows their reason. Fix what they asked, then the account owner selects [ui: Ask again]. Team members see the request but can't send it.

## Your company profile and team

Under [ui: Your company] in the menu:

- [ui: Company]: the [ui: Company profile] that schools see when you ask to recruit their students. Only the account owner can edit it. If you change the website, your company is no longer confirmed by your e-mail address.
- [ui: Team]: the account owner invites colleagues. Under [ui: Invite a colleague], fill in their [ui: Email], [ui: Name] and [ui: Job title], then select [ui: Send invite]. They join when they sign in with that address. [ui: Resend] sends a reminder, and [ui: Cancel invite] or [ui: Remove] takes their access away.

If you recruit for more than one company, choose the company at the top of each page.

## Ask to recruit at a school

1. Open [ui: Schools]. Under [ui: Find a school], type the [ui: School name] and select [ui: Search].
2. Select [ui: Request access]. Add a note about who you hire and the roles you recruit for. It helps the school decide.
3. Select [ui: Send request].

The school's career center approves, declines or blocks your request, and you're told when they decide. [ui: Your schools] lists each school with its status and the school's note. If a school declined, you can [ui: Request again]. A school that blocked you can't be asked again.

Once a school approves you, you can send it jobs, interview requests and challenges, see its career fairs, and browse its résumé book if it has one.
