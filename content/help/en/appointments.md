Open [Appointments](/student/appointments) from the menu.

## Book a time

1. Select [ui: Book].
2. Choose what you need help with. Each choice shows how long it takes and how you can meet: in person, by video or by phone.
3. Pick a [ui: Counselor], or leave [ui: Any available counselor]. Choose [ui: How would you like to meet?].
4. Pick a day and one of its open times. Only times when a counselor is free are shown. Use [ui: Next two weeks] to look further ahead.
5. Answer any questions the career center asks, then select [ui: Book appointment].

You get a confirmation, and the appointment shows under [ui: My appointments]. Select [ui: Add to calendar] to save it in your own calendar. Under the times, the page says how far ahead you can book and how late you can still change it online.

## If you can't make it

Under [ui: My appointments]:

- **Move it:** select [ui: Reschedule], pick another time with the same counselor, then select [ui: Move appointment].
- **Cancel it:** select [ui: Cancel], say why (your counselor sees this), then select [ui: Cancel appointment].

Close to the start, these buttons go away. Contact the career center instead.

## Drop-in hours and group sessions

- [ui: Drop-in]: when drop-in hours are open, select [ui: Join the line]. You see your place in line and about how long you'll wait. You can [ui: Leave the line].
- [ui: Group sessions]: workshops and group advising. Select [ui: Register]. If a session is full, select [ui: Join the waitlist]: you're registered automatically when a seat opens.

## After the meeting

Notes your counselor shares and the tasks you agreed on are under [ui: Notes & tasks]. Tick your tasks off as you finish them.

> Some appointments and sessions are only for students, or only for alumni, so you may see different choices from a friend.
