Mentors are volunteers. You choose your topics, how you meet and how many mentees you take. Mentoring on Edulexa is free.

## Where to start

- **Alumni of a school:** on your [alumni home page](/alumni), select [ui: Become a mentor].
- **The Edulexa community:** open [Mentoring](/mentors) and select [ui: Apply to mentor]. You need a verified e-mail address first, because mentors and mentees reach each other by e-mail.

Both open your mentor page, with [ui: Set up your mentor profile].

## Set up your profile

1. Fill in your [ui: Headline], one line on how you can help, and your [ui: Employer], [ui: Job title] and [ui: Industry].
2. Choose the [ui: Topics you can help with] and [ui: How you can meet] (video call, phone, in person or e-mail). You need at least one of each.
3. Set your [ui: Maximum active mentees], from 1 to 10. No one can ask you once you're full.
4. Add a [ui: Contact email for mentees] if you don't want to use your sign-in e-mail. It's shared only with mentees whose mentorship is active.
5. Select [ui: Save profile], then [ui: Submit for approval].

Your status shows at the top. Your school's career center reviews alumni mentors; in the Edulexa community, the Edulexa team reviews mentors, and your page says so. In the community, the Edulexa team also does what a career center does elsewhere in this guide. Once approved, your status is [ui: Listed] and people can find you. If the reviewer asks for changes, the status is [ui: Changes requested]: update your profile and submit it again.

Need a break? Select [ui: Pause new requests]. Your current mentees aren't affected. Select [ui: Resume new requests] when you're ready.

## Answer requests

Each request under [ui: Requests] shows the person's message, their goals, and what you have in common. Add a note if you like ([ui: Note to the student (optional)] at a school, [ui: Note to your mentee (optional)] in the Edulexa community), then select **Accept** or **Decline**.

When you accept, the mentorship becomes [ui: Active] and you both see each other's contact details. At some schools the career center confirms each match first, so it shows [ui: Waiting for career center] until they do.

## Look after your mentees

Under [ui: Your mentees], choose a mentee to open the mentorship. There you can:

- send private messages. The career center reads them only if one of you reports a problem;
- [ui: Log a session] after you meet. [ui: Private notes (only the two of you)] stay between you; a [ui: Summary for the career center (optional)] is shared with them;
- see the mentee's goals. Only the mentee changes them.

If no session has been logged for a while, the mentorship shows [ui: Check-in due].

When you're done, select [ui: Mark completed], or [ui: End early]. Then leave a rating and select [ui: Send feedback]. Only the career center sees it, not your mentee.

> If something feels wrong, select [ui: Report a problem]. You can also block the person, which ends the mentorship.

Edulexa advisors may also hand you career questions from people who aren't sure what to study. See [Guidance requests handed to you](/help/guidance-requests-for-you).
