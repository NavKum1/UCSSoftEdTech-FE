Everyone at your school is on the [ui: People] page of the career center. You can search by name or e-mail, filter by role, change someone's role, or remove them.

## Invite one person

1. Open [ui: People] and find [ui: Invite someone].
2. Enter their e-mail address and name.
3. Choose their role:
   - [ui: Faculty]: teachers. They create classes and assessments and decide results.
   - [ui: Counselor]: career staff who work with students.
   - [ui: Career center admin]: school admins. They can do everything, including inviting people.
   - [ui: Employer relations]: staff who work with employers.
   - [ui: Student]
4. Select [ui: Send invite].

Teachers and staff get an e-mail with where to sign in. Students added here aren't e-mailed: they hear about each assessment when a teacher gives it to their class.

## Import students from a spreadsheet

1. Under [ui: Import student roster], choose a CSV file.
2. Select [ui: Preview]. You see who would be added or updated, and any rows with problems.
3. Select [ui: Confirm import]. Rows with problems are skipped.

> Teachers usually import their own class lists instead, from the class page. See [Create a class and import its roster](/help/classes-and-rosters).

People sign in with the e-mail address you entered. When they sign in for the first time, their account is linked to your school.
