## Reports

Open [ui: Reports] from the [ui: Reports] menu. Choose a report:

- [ui: Engagement]: how many students used at least one service, first-time and returning, by role, graduation year, degree level and major.
- [ui: Service volume]: appointments, drop-in waits, group sessions, document review turnaround, mentoring and internships.
- [ui: Employers]: requests, approved employers, and how employers took part.
- [ui: Equity view]: use of services by groups such as work authorization and the details students chose to share about themselves.

Pick a [ui: Term], or choose [ui: Custom dates], and select [ui: Apply]. Each table can show a chart or the numbers. To keep a view you use often, save it under [ui: Save preset].

To share the numbers, select [ui: Export spreadsheet (CSV)], or [ui: Printable summary]. Small groups are hidden so no one can be picked out. A career admin sets the [ui: Smallest group shown in the equity view].

Employer relations staff see the employer report only.

## Outcomes

[ui: Outcomes] shows where your graduates went: a job, further study, service or still looking. Each placement shows where it came from, such as the student, the employer or your own research, and how well it's verified: [ui: Verified by employer], [ui: Confirmed by staff] or [ui: Self-reported].

- Select [ui: Confirm] when you've checked a student's own report.
- Use [ui: Add research entry] to add a placement you found yourself, with where you found it. Or record that a student is still looking.
- Students report their next step themselves on their [ui: My next step] page.

The [ui: Reports] tab shows, by graduation year and program, how many graduates have a known outcome and how many are placed. Select [ui: Export spreadsheet (CSV)] to download it.

> Placement records are part of the student's record. They're never shared with employers.
