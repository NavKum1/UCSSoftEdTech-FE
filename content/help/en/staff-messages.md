[ui: Messages & campaigns] is the career center's shared inbox. Every career staff member can use it.

## Answer a conversation

On [ui: Inbox], choose what to [ui: Show]: [ui: All open], [ui: Unassigned], [ui: Assigned to me], [ui: Closed] or [ui: Everything]. You can also search by subject, student or text. Select a conversation to read it.

In a conversation you can:

- Write your answer under [ui: Reply], attach a file if you need to, and select [ui: Send].
- Tick [ui: Internal note] to write something only staff can see, then select [ui: Add note].
- Choose who it's [ui: Assigned to], so your colleagues know who's answering.
- [ui: Close] it when it's done. If the student writes again, it opens again.

## Start a conversation

Select [ui: Message a student], find the student or alumnus by name, email or student number, then write a [ui: Subject] and [ui: Message] and select [ui: Send].

## Send an announcement

On [ui: Campaigns], select [ui: New campaign]. Choose email, an in-app notification or both, write the message, and choose [ui: Who receives it], for example by graduation date or major. Select [ui: Preview recipients] to check who gets it, then [ui: Send now], [ui: Schedule] it, or [ui: Save draft]. Students can unsubscribe from each category of email.

## Reported messages

When someone reports a message, it appears on [ui: Reports]. Read the conversation, then [ui: Mark resolved] with a note. Counselors and career admins resolve reports.
