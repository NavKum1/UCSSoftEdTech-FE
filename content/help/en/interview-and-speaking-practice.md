## A practice interview

Open [ui: Interview practice] from the [ui: Practice] menu.

1. [ui: 1. Choose a track], such as software, product management or sales.
2. [ui: 2. Choose an interviewer]. Each one says [ui: How tough the questions are].
3. Under [ui: 3. The job], enter the [ui: Job title] and paste the [ui: Job description or key requirements] from any job post. Or select one of the [ui: Example roles].
4. Enter [ui: Your name] and choose an [ui: Interviewer voice].
5. Select [ui: Start interview] and allow the microphone when your browser asks.

Talk as you would in a real interview. The interviewer asks follow-up questions, and your words show in the [ui: Live transcript]. Use [ui: Mute] to pause your microphone. When you're done, select [ui: End and get my scorecard]. If you reach the time limit or your connection drops, the interview ends and you still get your scorecard. [ui: Cancel] ends it without a scorecard.

The scorecard shows your [ui: Overall readiness], your [ui: Strengths], [ui: What to improve] and the whole [ui: Transcript]. You can copy or download the transcript. Select [ui: Practice again] for the same job, or [ui: Set up a new role].

> You pay only for the minutes you use. When you start, a whole session's worth of your voice minutes is set aside, up to the session limit shown in [Pricing and minutes](/pricing), so you can't run out partway. When the interview ends, the minutes you didn't use go back to your balance, and the scorecard shows how many you used. Each interview counts in whole minutes, rounded up: a 4 minute 10 second interview uses 5 minutes. If no answers were recorded, or the interview didn't connect, no minutes are used. It's for practice only: it doesn't give a credential.

## Speaking practice

[ui: Speaking practice] helps with how you speak, not what you say. It measures your pace (words a minute), filler words such as "um", your pauses, and how long you take to start. It doesn't judge emotion, stress or confidence.

1. The first time, read what's measured and select [ui: Turn on speaking practice].
2. Pick a [ui: Question], or write [ui: Or your own question].
3. Select [ui: Start answering], answer out loud, then select [ui: Stop and see feedback].

Your feedback shows straight away. [ui: Across your recent answers] shows your averages, and [ui: Your answers] lists each one.

- Your recording stays in your browser. To count words, your browser turns your answer into text, and Edulexa doesn't keep that text. Chrome, Edge and Safari can count words. In other browsers, only pauses and your start time are measured.
- Only you see your results. They're never shown to recruiters or institutes, or used in any score.
- Your measurements are kept for 180 days. Select [ui: Delete my answers] at any time, or [ui: Turn off speaking practice] to stop and delete them.

Speaking practice isn't offered in the EU and EEA, where the law limits voice analysis in education. It's also off when Edulexa can't tell which country you're in.
