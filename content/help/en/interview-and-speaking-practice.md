## A practice interview

Open [ui: Interview practice] from the [ui: Practice] menu.

1. Under [ui: 1. What you're practicing for], choose [ui: A job] and use [ui: Search for your job], for example "registered nurse", "electrician" or "accountant". Pick your job from the list. If it isn't there, select the button to use your own job title. Or choose a college admissions, scholarship or student visa interview.
2. For a job, choose [ui: 2. The round]: a [ui: Screening call], a [ui: Behavioral] interview about real situations from your past, or a [ui: Hiring manager] interview about the job's own tasks.
3. Under [ui: 3. Your résumé and the job ad], you can choose a résumé from your library and paste [ui: The job ad (optional)]. Then the questions are about your own experience and this job.
4. Under [ui: 4. The interviewer], choose [ui: Supportive] (hints when you're stuck), [ui: Standard], or [ui: Demanding] (follow-up questions and pushback), and an [ui: Interviewer voice].
5. Select [ui: Start interview] and allow the microphone when your browser asks.

[shot: member-practice-interview]

[ui: What this interview tests] shows the job's most important tasks and the four things you're scored on. The job information comes from public sources. See [Where job information comes from](/help/job-information-sources).

The student visa interview is practice only, not legal or immigration advice.

### What the AI interviewer sees

Before anything is sent to the AI, Edulexa takes out your name, contact details, address, date of birth, nationality, religion, visa status, salary and referees. The names of your employers, schools and places are replaced with stand-ins like [Employer 1], and exact dates become years. Open [ui: What the AI interviewer will see] to check it, and select [ui: Leave out] on any line you don't want used. Only you see what each stand-in means.

The interviewer hears what you say, so don't say your name or contact details during the interview.

### During the interview

Talk as you would in a real interview. The interviewer asks six to eight questions and follow-ups, and your words show in the [ui: Live transcript]. Use [ui: Mute] to pause your microphone. When you're done, select [ui: End and get my coaching]. If you reach the time limit or your connection drops, the interview ends and you still get your coaching. [ui: Cancel] ends it without coaching.

### Your coaching

After the interview you see [ui: Your result]: [ui: Strong], [ui: Interview-ready], [ui: Getting there] or [ui: Keep practicing], and a score for each of the four things you're scored on. Then [ui: Answer by answer] shows, for each question:

- [ui: You said]: your own words.
- [ui: What worked] and [ui: What was missing].
- [ui: A stronger version, in your words]. It's built only from what you said and your résumé. Highlighted parts are facts only you know, such as how many patients you had: fill them in with what really happened. Edulexa never writes facts you didn't give.

Select [ui: Try this question again] to answer one question again in a short call. Your try is coached and shows under your first answer, so you can see what changed. You can try each question three times, and each try uses practice minutes. Questions under [ui: Not reached] don't count against your result.

[shot: member-practice-coaching]

Your interviews are listed under [ui: Your interviews] on the practice page. Only you can see them. Your plan keeps them for a set time, shown next to each one. Under [ui: Keep them for] you can choose a shorter time, or [ui: A day after I see the coaching]. You can delete any interview at any time.

> You pay only for the minutes you use. When you start, a whole session's worth of your voice minutes is set aside, up to the session limit shown in [Pricing and minutes](/pricing), so you can't run out partway. When the interview ends, the minutes you didn't use go back to your balance. Each interview counts in whole minutes, rounded up: a 4 minute 10 second interview uses 5 minutes. If the interview didn't connect, or you end it within the first minute without answering, no minutes are used. It's for practice only: it doesn't give a credential, and nobody else sees it.

## Speaking practice

[ui: Speaking practice] helps with how you speak, not what you say. It measures your pace (words a minute), filler words such as "um", your pauses, and how long you take to start. It doesn't judge emotion, stress or confidence.

1. The first time, read what's measured and select [ui: Turn on speaking practice].
2. Pick a [ui: Question], or write [ui: Or your own question].
3. Select [ui: Start answering], answer out loud, then select [ui: Stop and see feedback].

Your feedback shows straight away. [ui: Across your recent answers] shows your averages, and [ui: Your answers] lists each one.

- Your recording stays in your browser. To count words, your browser turns your answer into text, and Edulexa doesn't keep that text. Chrome, Edge and Safari can count words. In other browsers, only pauses and your start time are measured.
- Only you see your results. They're never shown to recruiters or institutes, or used in any score.
- Your measurements are kept for 180 days. Select [ui: Delete my answers] at any time, or [ui: Turn off speaking practice] to stop and delete them.

Speaking practice isn't offered in the EU and EEA, where the law limits voice analysis in education. It's also off when Edulexa can't tell which country you're in.
