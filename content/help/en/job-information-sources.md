When you search for your job in [Interview practice](/help/interview-and-speaking-practice), Edulexa looks it up in a job library. For each job, the library lists its most important tasks, skills, knowledge and work styles. The interviewer uses them to ask about the real work of that job. If your job isn't in the library, you can practice with your own job title, and the questions are general for that kind of job.

## The sources

**O*NET®.** This service includes information from the O*NET 31.0 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the CC BY 4.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. Edulexa has modified all or some of this information. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications.

**ESCO.** This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. Edulexa has modified it: ESCO job names in Spanish and Arabic are matched to O*NET jobs.

## How Edulexa uses it

- The library is updated about once a year, after a new O*NET release.
- Job information is general. It describes the job in the United States, so some tasks may differ where you live.
- No information about you comes from these sources, and nothing about you is sent to them.
