[ui: Caseload] is for counselors and career center admins. It lists your students and flags the ones who may need a check-in. Each flag is a **signal**: a simple rule, such as a final-year student with no recent appointment, a message nobody has answered, a returned document review the student hasn't acted on, or an internship agreement that isn't signed yet.

## Find students

Under [ui: Filters], choose [ui: My caseload], [ui: All students], [ui: Unassigned pool] or a colleague's caseload. You can narrow the list by [ui: Signal] or [ui: Minimum severity], and sort it by [ui: Highest severity], [ui: Number of signals], [ui: Name] or [ui: Graduation date]. Select a student to see their signals and [ui: Recent activity].

## Follow up

- Most signals link to the page they come from, for example the student's internship.
- After you've been in touch, use [ui: Mark contacted]: choose [ui: How], add a note if you like, and select [ui: Save].
- To hide a signal for a while, select [ui: Snooze…] and give the number of days and a reason. Snoozes are recorded.
- If your school has turned on the AI copilot, [ui: Appointment prep brief] sums up the student before a meeting, and [ui: Draft outreach message] writes a message for you to edit. Nothing is sent until you tick [ui: I reviewed this message and want to send it] and select [ui: Send via messaging].

Notes about a meeting go on the appointment itself. See [Set your availability and manage appointments](/help/appointments-and-availability).

## Who is in your caseload

Career admins assign students with [ui: Caseload rules], by major, college or last-name range, or one by one. Students no rule matches stay in the unassigned pool. Admins also set when each signal appears, and turn the copilot on or off, in [ui: Signal & copilot settings].
