People who don't know which institute to ask, or which area or certification to choose, can send their question to Edulexa. As an Edulexa advisor, you answer them on [Guidance requests](/advisor/guidance). New requests, replies and hand-backs also come to the advisors by e-mail.

## Find a request

The list opens on [ui: Waiting for an advisor]. Use the list above the requests to show [ui: All] or another status: [ui: With a mentor], [ui: Answered], [ui: Institute suggested], [ui: Sent to an institute] or [ui: Resolved]. Choose a request to open it.

Each request shows the person's contact details, [ui: What they're unsure about:], their answers and any files they attached, and their counseling quiz result if they chose to share it.

## Reply

Write your reply and select [ui: Send]. The person gets it in Edulexa and by e-mail. If the request says reply-by-email isn't set up, people answer on their own page in Edulexa instead.

To leave a message for the other advisors and the mentor only, tick [ui: Note for staff only (the person doesn't see it)], then select [ui: Add note].

## Hand it to a community mentor

When you're stretched, choose a mentor under [ui: Hand to a community mentor] and select [ui: Hand over]. Only approved community mentors who have signed in are listed. The mentor gets a notification and an e-mail, and replies in Edulexa or by e-mail. See [Guidance requests handed to you](/help/guidance-requests-for-you).

To answer it yourself again, select [ui: Take back]. If the mentor hands it back, it's waiting for an advisor again.

## Suggest an institute

Under [ui: Suggest an institute], choose one of the institutes listed on Edulexa, add [ui: Why this institute (optional)], and select [ui: Suggest].

The person decides. They're asked by e-mail and on their own page, and nothing is sent to the institute unless they approve. If they approve, the request shows [ui: Sent to an institute] and the institute contacts them. If they decline, it's waiting for an advisor again.

## Finish

Select [ui: Mark resolved] when the person has what they need. [ui: Reopen] brings it back.

## Edulexa's questions

[Edulexa's forms](/advisor/questions) holds the questions people answer when they ask Edulexa for guidance. You change them the same way an institute changes its own forms: see [Build your guidance forms](/help/guidance-forms).
