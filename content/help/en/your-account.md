## Your account menu

Select the circle with your initials at the top right. It shows your name and e-mail address, and:

- [ui: Your account]: your name, e-mail address, sign-in, and where you study or work. See below.
- your other account pages. If you use Edulexa on your own, these are [ui: Pricing and minutes], [ui: Privacy and your data], [ui: Fairness self-ID] and [ui: Transparency report]. At a school, your privacy and notification pages are in your school's menu.
- [ui: Switch to], if you work in more than one area. See [More than one school or role](/help/switch-schools-and-roles).
- [ui: Appearance], for light or dark.
- [ui: Sign out].


## Language and spelling

At the top of the page, next to the light and dark button, is the language list. It shows the language you're using, such as [ui: English (US)].

- Choose [ui: English (US)] or **English (UK)** for British spelling ("color" or "colour"). If you haven't chosen, you get your school's choice, or US English.
- Spanish, Hindi and Arabic are being prepared. Each one is added to the list once a native speaker has checked it. If you see a language marked **(preview)**, it's a draft, and some words may be wrong.

Your choice applies to every page, and this browser remembers it.

## Light or dark

Select the sun or moon button at the top of the page. Or open your account menu and, under [ui: Appearance], choose [ui: Light], [ui: Dark] or [ui: System] (the same as your computer or phone).

## Your account page

Open your account menu and select [ui: Your account]. It shows your name and e-mail address, and every school or company you belong to on Edulexa, with your role there.

- **Your name:** select [ui: Change your name]. The sign-in page opens with your name; change it and save, and you come back here.
- **Your password:** next to [ui: Change your password], select [ui: Open]. Enter your new password twice. It needs at least 12 characters, and can't be your e-mail address or one of your last five passwords.
- **Two-step sign-in:** next to [ui: Set up an authenticator app], select [ui: Open], and scan the code with an app on your phone. From then on you enter a code from the app each time you sign in. Or choose [ui: Add a passkey] to sign in with your fingerprint, face or your device's PIN.

If you sign in with your school's account (your school's own sign-in page), your school manages your password. Change it the way your school tells you.

### Your e-mail address

Your e-mail address links you to your school, so you can't change it yourself. To ask for a change, see [Get help and contact support](/help/help-and-support).

Forgot your password and can't sign in? Use **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page. See [Sign in and sign out](/help/sign-in).
