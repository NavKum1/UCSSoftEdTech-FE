A résumé book holds the résumés students chose to share with the employers their school approved. A school's book shows in [ui: Résumé books] once the school approves your company and opens its book.

1. Open [ui: Résumé books] under [ui: Hiring] and select a school.
2. Narrow the list by [ui: Program], [ui: Degree] and [ui: Graduation year], or type [ui: Words in the résumé]. Then select [ui: Search].
3. Select [ui: Open résumé] to download it.

Each résumé you open, and each search that finds one, is recorded and shown to the student and their school.

Students can leave the book at any time, and the school can take a résumé out. Either way, it disappears from your list straight away.

If no books show, ask more schools for approval on [ui: Schools]. See [Set up your company and ask to recruit at a school](/help/employer-getting-started).
