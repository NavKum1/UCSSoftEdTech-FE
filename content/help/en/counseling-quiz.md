The counseling quiz asks your members a few questions and suggests the career area that fits them best, with your courses for that area. Open [ui: Counseling quiz] under [ui: Forms]. The page has three tabs: [ui: Questions], [ui: Courses] and [ui: Inquiries].

## Questions

Each member answers a set of questions drawn at random from your list. The top of the tab says how many questions are in use and how many each member answers.

Until you save your own, your institute uses Edulexa's standard questions. To change them:

1. Change a [ui: Question], or select [ui: Add a question].
2. For each answer, choose its area under [ui: Points to area] and its [ui: Weight]. Select [ui: Answer] under the answers to add another.
3. Untick [ui: In use] to leave a question out without deleting it, or select [ui: Delete].
4. Select [ui: Save].

Members never see the areas or the weights. Give every area the same number of answers and weights, so the quiz doesn't favor one.

To go back to Edulexa's standard questions, select **Use Edulexa's standard questions**. Your own questions are removed.

## Courses

Your courses are suggested to your members with their quiz results, best match first. Select [ui: Add a course], then fill in its [ui: Name], [ui: Description] and [ui: Area (matched to quiz results)], and, if you like, its [ui: Qualification], [ui: Length], [ui: Next intake] and highlights. Tick [ui: Shown to members] and select [ui: Save course].

Each course shows [ui: Shown] or [ui: Hidden]. Select [ui: Edit] or [ui: Remove] to change it. Inquiries already sent about a removed course are kept.

Only add accreditation you can show members when they ask.

## Inquiries

When a member asks about one of your courses, they're listed under [ui: Inquiries], with the course, the area of their result and their question. [ui: Referred by staff] means someone else sent them. Each inquiry also arrives as a lead in your [admissions leads](/institute/dashboard), where your team follows it up.
