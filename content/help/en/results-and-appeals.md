When your teacher has decided, the assessment page under [ui: My assessments] shows your result: your score for each part of the rubric, your overall score out of 100, and your teacher's comment.

## If you passed

If the assessment issues credentials, a signed credential is added to your wallet. Select [ui: View your credential] to see it, or open [ui: Your wallet] for all of them. Each has a public page you can share; anyone can check it's real.

## If you're asked to resubmit

You can hand in again, even after the due date. Select [ui: Start attempt 2] (or the next number). Your teacher's feedback on the attempt before stays on the page while you work.

## If you think the decision is wrong

You can appeal once, within 14 days. Select [ui: Appeal], explain why the decision should change, and select [ui: Send appeal]. Another teacher of your class, or a school admin, looks at it and answers on the same page. They either keep the decision or change it.

> Once you start a new attempt, you can no longer appeal the decision on the one before.
