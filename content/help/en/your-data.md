## At your school

Your school's privacy page is in the career center under your account: [Privacy](/student/privacy).

- **How your data is used:** uses that need your consent are listed, each with an on/off switch. Withdrawing stops the use from then on.
- Under [ui: Your data rights], at [ui: What do you want?] choose a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, add any details, and select [ui: Send request]. Your school's career office answers within the time shown.
- Under [ui: Your data requests], follow your requests, and [ui: Download] your copy when it's ready.

If you're under the age your country sets, a parent or guardian may need to agree first.

## Your Edulexa account

To download everything in your account, or delete it, go to [Privacy and your data](/settings/privacy). Deleting your account removes your own activity. Records your school holds about your class work, such as your attempts and documents, are the school's: your deletion sends the school a request to erase them, and the school completes it.
