[Résumés](/resumes) keeps all your résumés in one place.

## Add a résumé

Select [ui: Upload résumé] and choose a PDF or Word (.docx) file, up to 5 MB. Edulexa reads its text. If it can't (a scanned page, for example), the résumé shows [ui: Couldn't read text]. You can still send it as it is.

Or select [ui: Build a résumé], then [ui: Start from your record] or [ui: Start blank]. Edulexa lays it out and makes the PDF. Lines from your verified results print with a Verified mark.

[shot: resumes-library]

## Keep it up to date

- [ui: Upload a new version] when you change it. The newest version is the one you send. Older ones are kept: [ui: Versions and where they went] lists them and where you sent each one.
- [ui: AI feedback] suggests stronger lines. They're only suggestions: change what helps, in your own words.
- [ui: Archive] puts away one you don't use; restore it from [ui: Archived] at any time.

To see how well a résumé fits a job posting, select [ui: Check against a job].

## Use it when you apply

[Job applications](/help/campus-jobs-and-interviews), [document reviews](/help/document-review) and interview sign-ups offer [ui: From your library]. Each keeps its own copy of the version you send. A résumé you upload in one of those forms is saved to your library too.

Some schools take only résumés the career center approved. Send yours for [document review](/help/document-review) first. Once it's approved, it shows [ui: Approved by your school].

If your school has a résumé book, select [ui: Add to the résumé book] at the bottom of the page. Employers your school approved can then find your résumé, and you see how often they open it. You can [ui: Leave the résumé book] at any time.

> If your school uses another résumé tool, its forms don't take résumés from here. Your library still works for everything else.
