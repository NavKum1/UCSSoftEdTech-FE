Every day there are two short drills: an incident drill and a code review. Both are in the [ui: Practice] menu, under [ui: Incident drills] and [ui: Code review drills]. The [Daily practice](/daily) page shows both, with your streak and points.

## Points and your streak

- Your **first try** at each drill earns points. You can do a drill again, but repeats don't earn points.
- The answers and the reasons are shown as soon as you submit.
- [ui: Streak]: the days in a row you've practiced. Every 7 days in a row earn a streak freeze (you can hold 2). A freeze covers one missed day for you.
- [ui: This week] starts again each week, on the day the page shows. [ui: All time] counts every point since you started.

## The incident drill

About 10 minutes. A service is failing, and you work out why.

1. Choose your [ui: Track] and [ui: Difficulty], then select [ui: Show today's drill].
2. Select [ui: Open the drill]. The clock starts now.
3. Read the [ui: Symptoms], [ui: Metrics] and [ui: Logs].
4. Choose the [ui: Root cause] and, under [ui: What do you do now?], the safe fix. Then select [ui: Submit].

The right cause and the right fix are worth 50 points each, with 20 bonus points if both are right within 5 minutes. Afterwards you see every cause, the risk of each action, and a [ui: Post-mortem].

To practice on the tools you use, select [ui: Generate a drill for your own stack].

## The code review

About 3 minutes. A change to some code has one defect in it.

1. Select [ui: Open the pull request]. The clock starts now.
2. Select the line with the defect. A line next to it counts too.
3. Choose the [ui: Kind of defect], then [ui: Your decision]: [ui: Approve], [ui: Request changes] or [ui: Comment only].
4. Select [ui: Submit review].

The right line is worth 50 points, the right call 30 and the right kind of defect 20. Get the line and the call right within 3 minutes for 20 bonus points.

## System design duels

In [ui: System design duels], you design a system against the clock, and your design is scored against a practice bot. The bots aren't people, and their scores are fixed.

1. Under [ui: Start a duel], choose a [ui: Scenario] and an [ui: Opponent], then start. The page shows the goals and how designs are scored.
2. Add parts from [ui: Components], set how many [ui: Replicas] each needs, and join them with [ui: Connect].
3. Select [ui: Submit design] before the time runs out.

You see each check your design passed or missed, and your new rating. Duels don't earn daily points.

## Hard conversations and failover

[ui: Leadership practice] and [ui: Failover practice] are short scenarios. In each turn, choose your response. At the end you see how close each choice was to the strongest response. Only you see your result. Select [ui: Try again] or [ui: Other scenarios], and find earlier tries under [ui: Your past attempts].

## The leaderboard

[ui: Leaderboard] ranks members on graded results only: [ui: This week], [ui: All time] and [ui: Verified challenges]. Names show as a first name and last initial. To leave the leaderboard, switch off [ui: Show me to other members]. You still see your own rank.
