Your career center home page shows your school's name, the role you're signed in as, how many people of each role your school has, and a [ui: Go to] list of every page you can open.

The menu at the top groups the pages:

- [ui: Students]: appointments, caseload, document review, interview schedules, mentoring and messages.
- [ui: Employers and jobs]: employers, career fairs, internships, placements, campus drives and the résumé book.
- [ui: Reports]: outcomes and reports.
- [ui: Setup]: people, your calendar connection, settings and more.
- [ui: Privacy]: privacy requests and the privacy log.

The bell at the top opens your notifications. If you work at more than one school, choose the school at the top of the page.

## What each role can do

- **Career center admins** can do everything below. They also invite people, change the school's settings, and set the rules for each area, such as appointment types, caseload rules and drive rules.
- **Counselors** handle appointments, their caseload, document review and messages, and review internships for credit. They can look at campus drives and interview schedules, but they don't approve employers, drives or schedules.
- **Employer relations** staff approve employers, campus drives and interview schedules, look after the résumé book, answer messages, and see the employer report. Appointments, caseload and document review are for counselors and admins.

## Where to start

- **You meet students:** set the hours students can book. See [Set your availability and manage appointments](/help/appointments-and-availability).
- **You work with employers:** your home page shows how many [ui: Employer requests] are waiting. See [Approve employers](/help/employers-and-approvals).
- **You're an admin:** invite your staff from [ui: People] and give each one the right role.
