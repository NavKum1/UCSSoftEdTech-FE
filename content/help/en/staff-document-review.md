Students send a résumé, cover letter, personal statement or LinkedIn summary for review. Their requests wait in [ui: Document review] for counselors and career center admins.

## Pick up a request

On [ui: Queue], filter by [ui: Status], [ui: Document type], [ui: Assignee] or [ui: Waiting at least (days)], or tick [ui: Overdue only]. Open a request, select [ui: Claim], then [ui: Start review].

Once a request is yours, you can hand it to a colleague with [ui: Assign to]. Career admins can assign any request. Use [ui: Mark urgent] when the student's deadline is close.

## Write your review

- Select a passage in the submitted text and choose [ui: Comment on selection], or write a [ui: General comment]. Then select [ui: Add comment].
- Rate each line of the [ui: Checklist]: [ui: Strong], [ui: Satisfactory], [ui: Needs work] or [ui: Not applicable].
- Write the [ui: Overall summary]. You need it before you can return the review, and the student sees it exactly as you write it.
- If you like, upload a marked-up copy under [ui: Annotated file].

Select [ui: Save draft] to finish later, or [ui: Return to student]. The student is told and can send a new version. Earlier versions stay on the page, with your comments.

If your school asks for an approved résumé for campus applications, you'll see [ui: Approve this résumé] when you return one. Tick it to approve that version.

## Metrics and settings

[ui: Metrics] shows how many reviews each reviewer returned, how fast, how many were on time, and how students rated them. Select [ui: Export spreadsheet (CSV)] to download it. Career admins set the review target and the checklists under [ui: Settings].

> Reviews are part of the student's education record. They're never shared with employers.
