## A class's results

The class's page for an assessment shows its [ui: Results] from your decisions: how many passed, were asked to resubmit or didn't pass, the average and middle scores, and the average for each rubric criterion. Use [ui: Download results (spreadsheet)] for a spreadsheet of every student.

## Credentials

When a student passes an assessment that issues credentials, a signed credential goes into their wallet, with a public page anyone can use to check it.

If a credential was issued by mistake, open the student's evidence and use [ui: Withdraw the credential], with the reason. Its page then shows it's no longer valid.

## Grades in your LMS

If your school uses Canvas, Moodle or Blackboard, Edulexa can send each decision's score to the course gradebook (LTI 1.3). Your school sets up the connection once with Edulexa's team. Then:

1. In your course, add the Edulexa activity and pick this assessment.
2. Each student opens it once from the course, so the gradebook knows who they are.
3. Every decision sends the score to the gradebook.

The class's page shows each student's grade: sent, waiting to send, not sent, or not linked yet (the student hasn't opened it from the course). If one wasn't sent, select [ui: Send again].
