## Where each candidate stands

At the top of [ui: Candidates], the tiles count your candidates: [ui: Awaiting review], [ui: In progress], [ui: Advanced], [ui: On hold] and [ui: Declined]. Select a tile to see only those candidates. Select it again, or [ui: Show all], to see everyone.

The [ui: Assessments] tab lists each candidate you sent an assessment to:

- [ui: Stage scores]: the written (W), coding (C) and voice (V) scores, in percent.
- [ui: Progress]: the stage they're on, or [ui: All stages complete], and what happened to their link.
- [ui: Composite]: the overall score once every stage is done. [ui: Needs scoring] means it isn't scored yet.

Select [ui: Details] to see the stage scores, an AI-written summary of the interview with the strengths and gaps it noticed, and the voice defense transcript. All of this is for reference only.

## Record your decision

1. Select [ui: Record decision].
2. Choose [ui: Advanced to the next step], [ui: Hire] or [ui: Not moving forward].
3. Add [ui: Notes for your team (optional)] and select [ui: Save decision].

This records your decision in Edulexa. Nothing is sent to the candidate. To change it, select [ui: Change decision].

## Coding challenge submissions

The [ui: Coding challenge submissions] tab lists work handed in for your coding challenges, with the score and [ui: Integrity signals]: [ui: Signals to review] or [ui: No signals raised]. Signals are reasons for a conversation, not proof. Select [ui: Evidence pack] to review the work and decide. See [Review an evidence pack and decide](/help/recruiter-evidence-packs).

## Proctored sessions

Open [ui: Proctored sessions] under [ui: Review]. It lists proctored sessions with the events Edulexa recorded, such as switching tabs, leaving full screen, pasting text, or a camera or microphone changing. There's no gaze or face detection.

Each session starts with an [ui: Integrity score] of 100. Each event lowers it, by an amount set only by the kind of event. The status is [ui: Clean], [ui: Suspicious] or [ui: Flagged], and a very low score shows [ui: Needs review]. No one is disqualified automatically.

1. Use the tabs to choose which sessions to see, for example [ui: Needs review].
2. Select [ui: Open] to see the [ui: Session log], with each event and its time.
3. Write [ui: Your review (recorded with your name)] and select [ui: Record review].

The session then shows [ui: Reviewed]. A [ui: Flagged] session is shown as [ui: Suspicious] from then on.

## Challenge results

[ui: Challenge results] under [ui: Review] shows how candidates did on each coding challenge: the number of [ui: Candidates], the [ui: Median tests passed], the spread of each candidate's best result, and how many are [ui: For review]. It counts graded results only: the share of hidden tests passed, or a [ui: Mentor-adjusted score].
