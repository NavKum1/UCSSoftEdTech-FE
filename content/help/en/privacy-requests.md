Students ask for a copy of their data, a correction or deletion from their privacy page. A request also arrives when a student deletes their Edulexa account: it lists the records your school holds about them, such as class attempts, documents and roster entries.

Requests are on the career center's privacy page, under [ui: Privacy requests] and [ui: Request queue]. Each shows when it's due; overdue ones are marked. Your school sets how many days you have under [ui: Days to answer requests].

## Handle a request

Open the request and select [ui: Start]. Then:

- **A copy of their data:** use [ui: Preview export] to check it, then [ui: Release export to the student]. They download it from their privacy page.
- **A correction:** make the change, then [ui: Mark corrected] with a [ui: Note to the student].
- **Deletion:** use [ui: Show erasure plan] to see exactly what would be deleted, table by table. To go ahead, [ui: Type ERASE to confirm] and select [ui: Erase and complete]. Erasure can't be undone. Records the law requires your school to keep are kept, as the plan shows.

To refuse a request, use [ui: Decline] with a note explaining why. Every step is kept in the request's audit trail.
