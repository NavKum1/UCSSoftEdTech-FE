## Internships for credit

Students register an internship with its documents. Open [ui: Internships] from the [ui: Employers and jobs] menu. The [ui: Dashboard] shows what needs attention, such as agreements not fully signed and overdue evaluations. The [ui: Review queue] lists every internship.

### Approve an internship

Open an internship. Under [ui: Career-center review] you'll see whether faculty have approved it and whether the required documents are in. For F-1 students whose program needs it, record the CPT dates first.

When everything is ready, select [ui: Approve]. The learning agreement is created and sent for signatures. To turn it down, select [ui: Decline] with a comment the student sees.

Counselors and career admins approve internships. Career admins also set up the credit programs on the [ui: Programs] tab.

### Work with the site supervisor

Supervisors don't need an account. Under [ui: Site supervisor links], send them a link to confirm and sign the agreement, approve hours, or fill in an evaluation. Each link does one job and expires after 30 days. When the internship is under way, [ui: Open final evaluations now] asks the supervisor for the final evaluation.

## Placements

[ui: Placements] is your school's own record of campus drives, rounds, offers and packages. Companies don't post jobs here, and students don't apply here. Jobs employers send you are under [Campus drives](/help/jobs-drives-and-interviews).

- [ui: Record a drive] with the criteria the company published, then open it to see who's eligible and record each round.
- Add offers from a drive on its page. Use [ui: Record an off-campus offer] for the rest.
- Career admins import [ui: Academic records], which the eligibility lists need.

Students see their own results and offers on [ui: My placement record]. Accepted offers become placements on the [Outcomes](/help/reports-and-outcomes) page.
