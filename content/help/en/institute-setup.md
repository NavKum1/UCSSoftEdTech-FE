Your institute's area has three menus: [ui: Leads], [ui: Forms] and [ui: Settings]. Admins see all three. Counselors see only the admissions leads, where they work with the people who asked about your courses.

## The four setup steps

Open [ui: School setup] under [ui: Settings]. Each step can be skipped; come back from the checklist on your [admissions leads](/institute/dashboard) page.

1. [ui: Your school]: what kind of organization you are, your [ui: Logo] and brand color, your [ui: Website], the e-mail address for families' replies, and the [ui: Spelling on your forms, reports and e-mails]. Your logo and color go on your forms, the reports people receive and your e-mails. Select [ui: Save and continue].
2. [ui: Your first form]: pick a template made for schools like yours. It's published with your logo and color. You can change its questions later. See [Build your guidance forms](/help/guidance-forms).
3. [ui: Your team]: invite your counselors and admins.
4. [ui: Your website]: copy [ui: The form's link], or the code for your website. If someone else runs your website, enter their e-mail address and we'll send them the code and the instructions.

Select [ui: Finish and open the dashboard] at the end. Until every step is done, a setup checklist shows on your admissions leads page. Each step ticks itself when it's done. Select [ui: Hide] to put it away.

## Who can sign in

[ui: Staff access] lists who can sign in for your institute, and as what:

- [ui: Institute admin]: everything at your institute, including staff access and settings;
- [ui: Counselor]: leads, follow-ups, meetings and guidance results;
- [ui: Faculty]: teaching tools for their own courses and students.

Enter a [ui: Work e-mail] and choose a [ui: Role], then select [ui: Send invitation]. New people get an e-mail to set their password. People who already have an account get access at once. Changes take effect the next time the person signs in. [ui: History of changes] shows who changed what.

## Your admissions team and templates

[ui: Admissions settings] has three tabs:

- [ui: Team]: the people who can own leads and get follow-up reminders. Add each one with the e-mail they sign in with, so [ui: My leads] finds their leads.
- [ui: Email templates]: the e-mails your team sends often. Names, the course and your institute are filled in for each person when it's sent.
- [ui: Connected apps]: [ui: SMS and WhatsApp], and [ui: Send leads to your own systems] if you also keep your leads in another system. That part is for your IT team: it names the address and the key they must set up.

## Bring your leads over

On [ui: Import leads], choose a [ui: Spreadsheet (CSV file)] exported from your old system, such as VirtualAdviser. An Excel file? In Excel, choose File → Save As → CSV (UTF-8) first. Then:

1. Say [ui: Where the leads came from]. It becomes their source and a tag, so you can find them.
2. Choose how to read dates such as 03/04/2026, and what to do with someone who is already in your leads (the same e-mail address or phone number): add the row to their lead as a note, or leave it out.
3. Check [ui: What each column is], then look at [ui: What will happen]. Nothing is saved until you import, and nothing is sent to the people you import.

[ui: Past imports] lists each import. Select [ui: Undo] to take one back.

[ui: Billing] shows your plan.
