The viva is a short oral examination about the work you handed in. An AI examiner asks a few questions, one at a time: why you made a choice, what a part of your work means, or how you'd answer an objection. It doesn't give hints or scores. Your teacher reads the whole viva and decides your result.

## By voice

Select [ui: Start the viva by voice] and allow your browser to use the microphone. Answer each question out loud; you see what's said as you go. When you've answered the last question, select [ui: End the viva and hand in].

Find a quiet place, and give yourself the time the assessment page shows.

## In writing

Select [ui: Answer in writing], or it's offered automatically when the viva is in writing. Type each answer and select [ui: Send answer]; the last one is [ui: Send my last answer and hand in].

## Tips

- Explain in your own words. Spelling, grammar and accent don't count; understanding does.
- Talk about your own work, in detail. It's fine to say what you'd do differently.
- Don't read your work out. Your teacher can see when answers are mostly copied from it.

## If voice doesn't work

If the call doesn't connect, or nothing you said was kept, use [ui: Voice didn't work? Answer in writing]. Your teacher sees that you answered in writing after voice failed, so it doesn't count against you.

After the viva, you may be asked [ui: Two quick questions] about the assessment. Your answers are anonymous. Then your teacher decides: see [Your result and appeals](/help/results-and-appeals).
