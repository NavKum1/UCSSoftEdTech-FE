[Messages](/student/messages) holds your private conversations with the career center. While you have an active mentorship, your conversation with your mentor is here too. Only you and the people in a conversation can read it.

## Start a conversation

1. Select [ui: Message the career center].
2. Write a [ui: Subject] and your [ui: Message]. You can attach one file: PDF, Word, PNG, JPEG or text, up to 10 MB.
3. Select [ui: Send].

Someone at the career center answers in the same conversation. You get a notification when they do.

## Reply and keep track

Open a conversation from the list, write under [ui: Reply], and select [ui: Send]. Each of your messages shows [ui: Sent], then [ui: Seen] once it's been read. To find an older message, use [ui: Search messages].

When your question is answered, select [ui: Close conversation]. If you write in it again later, it opens again.

> Never send passwords, or numbers from official papers such as a passport, in messages.

A conversation with a mentor takes new messages only while the mentorship is active. After it ends, you can still read it. See [Find a mentor](/help/mentoring).

## If a message is wrong

If a message is rude, unsafe or spam, select [ui: Report] next to it, choose a reason, and select [ui: Send report]. The career center reviews every report.

## Announcements by email

The career center also emails announcements to groups of students. Each one has a link at the bottom to stop that kind of announcement. You still get emails about your own conversations and appointments.
