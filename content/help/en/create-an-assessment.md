Every assessment works the same way: students hand in their own work, then explain and defend it in a viva, and you decide the result. The template sets what students hand in and gives you a starting rubric.

## Choose a template

Open [ui: Assessments]. Under [ui: New assessment], choose one:

**Any subject**

- [ui: Essay], [ui: Report], [ui: Lab report] or [ui: Presentation]: students hand in a PDF, Word or PowerPoint file, or paste their text. The viva asks about what's in it.

**Code and exams**

- [ui: Project defense]: students hand in their own GitHub repository or a .zip of their project.
- [ui: Coding task]: students solve a coding task in the browser workspace.
- [ui: Written exam plus viva]: students take a written exam, then explain their answers.

Give it a [ui: Title] and select [ui: Continue]. For a coding task or an exam, you then choose which task or exam it uses.

[shot: new-assessment]

## Fill in the details

- [ui: Instructions for students]: what to hand in, what the viva covers, and anything they should prepare.
- The [ui: Rubric] and the [ui: Viva]: see [Set the rubric and the viva](/help/rubric-and-viva).
- [ui: Issue a credential when a student passes]: on by default; turn it off for practice or homework.

Select [ui: Save changes] as you go. When it's ready, select [ui: Publish]. Then give it to a class: see [Give an assessment to a class](/help/give-to-a-class).

> Once students have started, the task, the rubric and the viva are locked, so everyone is assessed the same way. You can still change the title, the instructions and the credential setting. To change more, copy it into a new assessment.

You can [ui: Archive] an assessment you no longer use, and [ui: Restore] it later.
