The bell at the top of the page shows how many notifications you haven't read. Select it to see them all. You'll also find [ui: Notifications] under [ui: More settings] on [Your account](/help/your-account).

## What you're told about

Things that are new for you or need you: for example, a new assessment, a decision on your work, a booked or cancelled appointment, or a reviewed document.

Select [ui: Open] to go to it, or [ui: Mark read]. Each notification also comes by e-mail, unless you turn that off.

## Choose what you get

At the bottom of the notifications page, under [ui: Notification preferences], each kind of notification you've had has two boxes: [ui: In app] and [ui: Email]. Untick a box to stop that kind; it's saved at once.

> On a phone with data saving on, the preferences load only when you ask: select **Load notification settings**.

## Announcements from your career center

Career centers send announcements by e-mail to groups of students and graduates. Each one has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. It opens [ui: Email preferences], where you can stop that kind of announcement, or select [ui: Unsubscribe from all campaign emails]. E-mails about your own appointments, conversations and requests still come.

## WhatsApp and text messages

If your school has turned them on, students can get short reminders on WhatsApp or by text message. Open [ui: Chat & SMS], enter your mobile number, agree, and select [ui: Send me a code]. Enter the code and select [ui: Confirm]. To stop, select [ui: Turn off WhatsApp] (or SMS), or reply STOP to any message.

## Without a school

If you use Edulexa on your own, or as a recruiter, mentor or admissions counselor, the bell is at the top of your pages too. Your notifications page works the same way, with the same choices.
