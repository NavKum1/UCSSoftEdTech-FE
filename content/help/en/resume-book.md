The résumé book holds résumés your students chose to share with the employers your school approved. Employers browse it by program, degree and graduation year.

## Open the résumé book

A career admin opens it in [ui: Settings], under [ui: Résumés]: tick [ui: Open the résumé book] and select [ui: Save settings]. This option shows only while [ui: Students use Edulexa's résumé tools] is ticked.

Students then decide for themselves. Each one who wants to adds a résumé from their own [ui: Résumés] page and agrees to share it. If your school asks for approved résumés, they can only add one a reviewer approved in [document review](/help/staff-document-review). Students can leave the book at any time.

## See who's in it

Open [ui: Résumé book] from the [ui: Employers and jobs] menu. For each student it shows their program, when they added their résumé, and how many times employers opened it, with the last date.

Every résumé an employer opens is recorded in your school's privacy log.

## Take a résumé out

If a résumé shouldn't be there, select [ui: Take out] and write a [ui: Reason for the student]. Employers stop seeing it at once. The student sees your reason and can add a résumé again.

Career admins, counselors and employer relations staff can all see the résumé book and take résumés out.

## Which employers see it

Only employers your school approved. See [Approve employers](/help/employers-and-approvals).
