## Your résumé library

[ui: Résumés] keeps all your résumés in one place.

- Select [ui: Upload résumé] and choose a PDF or Word (.docx) file, up to 5 MB. Edulexa reads its text. If it can't (a scanned page, for example), the résumé shows [ui: Couldn't read text], and Job check and Cover letters can't use it. You can still download it and send it as it is.
- Or select [ui: Build a résumé], then [ui: Start from your record] (your name and e-mail address) or [ui: Start blank].

For an uploaded résumé:

- [ui: Upload a new version] when you change it. Older versions are kept under [ui: Versions and where they went].
- [ui: AI feedback] suggests stronger lines. They're only suggestions: change what helps, in your own words.
- [ui: Turn into a built résumé] to edit it here.
- [ui: Archive] puts away one you don't use. Restore it from [ui: Archived] at any time.

## Build a résumé

Edulexa lays it out for you. Add sections and lines, then select [ui: Save version]. Each save keeps a version you can [ui: Download PDF] or [ui: Download Word].

Under [ui: Verified evidence] are lines drafted from your credentials. Select [ui: Add to résumé] to use one. It prints with a Verified mark that links to the proof. You can change the wording, but changing a number removes the mark.

Under [ui: AI help]:

- [ui: Get feedback on each line].
- [ui: Tailor to a job]: choose a saved job check, select [ui: Suggest changes for this job], tick the ones you want, and select [ui: Apply the ones I chose]. Then look them over and save a version.

Each AI request uses one of your AI uses for the month. The page shows how many you have left.

## Check it against a job

[ui: Job check] shows where your résumé mentions what a job asks for.

1. Select [ui: New job check]. Enter the [ui: Job title], the [ui: Company (optional)] and the [ui: Job description].
2. Under [ui: Requirements, one per line], list what the job asks for, or select [ui: Suggest requirements] to have the AI copy them from the description. Check the list.
3. Under [ui: Check this résumé], choose a résumé, then select [ui: Save and check].

Each requirement shows [ui: Mentioned] or [ui: Not found]. There's no score: a mention isn't proof, and add something missing only if it's true.

## Draft a cover letter

1. In [ui: Cover letters], select [ui: Draft a cover letter].
2. Choose the résumé under [ui: Based on this résumé], and the job under [ui: For this job]: a saved job check, or [ui: A job I'll paste below].
3. Select [ui: Draft with AI].

The draft uses only what your résumé says. Read it closely and make it yours, then select [ui: Save version]. Download it with [ui: Download PDF] or [ui: Download Word].
