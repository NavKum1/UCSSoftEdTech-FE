## Ask for an interview day

1. Open [ui: Interview schedules] under [ui: Hiring] and select [ui: Request a schedule].
2. Under [ui: Position], choose the [ui: School], and fill in the [ui: Position title] and a [ui: Job posting link] to where students read and apply for the job on your own job platform.
3. Under [ui: Days and format], set each day's [ui: Date], [ui: Start] and [ui: End], a lunch break if you like, the [ui: Interview length (minutes)] and the [ui: Break between interviews (minutes)]. For more than one interview at a time, raise [ui: Rooms needed]. For [ui: Virtual interviews], add the [ui: Video meeting link]. List your [ui: Interviewers], one per line.
4. Under [ui: Who can sign up], choose the [ui: Sign-up type]: [ui: Open to qualified students], [ui: Pre-select list], or [ui: Pre-select with alternates]. Add any [ui: Work authorization] requirement, majors, graduation dates and degree levels. Students confirm their work authorization themselves; you don't receive their status.
5. Select [ui: Send request].

The career center approves or declines your request, assigns rooms and publishes it. Students can then sign up for a time. [ui: My schedules] shows each request's status and any note from the career center.

## Invite students and run the day

Select [ui: Candidates and details] on a schedule.

- **For a pre-select list:** paste the [ui: Student emails], for example a column copied from a spreadsheet, and select [ui: Invite students]. Only students of that school are invited, and you see their details once they accept. With alternates, choose whether each one is invited as primary or alternate under [ui: Invite as].
- [ui: Interviews] lists each booked time, with the student's details and their résumé to download.
- On the day, select [ui: Check in] or [ui: Mark no-show] for each student, and choose the [ui: Outcome]: [ui: Offer], [ui: No offer] or [ui: Pending].
- [ui: Withdraw schedule] cancels the whole schedule. Booked students are told it was cancelled.

## Challenges

A challenge invites students to a skills exercise built on one of your coding tasks. It isn't a job posting: you can link to your own job page, and students choose whether to share their results with you. Your company needs approval from at least one school first.

1. Open [ui: Challenges] under [ui: Hiring] and select [ui: New challenge].
2. Give it a [ui: Title] and choose the [ui: Assessment]: one of your company's work trials that passed its check (your recruiters make these on the recruiter portal), one of your company's private coding tasks, or a public one.
3. Set when it [ui: Opens] and [ui: Closes]. If you like, add who can join, the [ui: Maximum participants], a [ui: Link to your job page] and a [ui: Prize or recognition].
4. Select [ui: Save draft].
5. Select [ui: Manage], tick the schools that approved your company, and select [ui: Publish]. Each school's career center reviews it before students see it.

Under [ui: Manage], the results button shows [ui: Participants sharing results], with each student's score, tests, integrity signal and defense, and their [ui: Evidence pack]. Students who don't share aren't shown.

To get in touch with a student, select [ui: Invite to talk], write a [ui: Message] and add a link if you like, such as a scheduling page, then select [ui: Send invitation]. There's no reply thread: the student decides whether to contact you.

When it's over, select [ui: Close challenge].
