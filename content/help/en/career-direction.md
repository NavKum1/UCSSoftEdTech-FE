## The career quiz

Open [ui: Counseling quiz] from the [ui: Guidance] menu.

1. Select [ui: Start the quiz]. The page says how many questions there are and about how long it takes. There are no right or wrong answers, and you don't need any experience.
2. Answer each question, then select [ui: Next]. Use [ui: Back] to change an answer.
3. On the last question, select [ui: See my result].

Need a break? Select [ui: Leave (your answers are kept)], and later [ui: Continue where you left off].

Your result names the area of tech that suits you, and often a second one. [ui: How your answers split] shows how often you picked each area, and [ui: Why this result] lists the answers that led there. You also see [ui: Roles people start in] and [ui: Good things to learn first].

Your result is saved. You can take the quiz again after the time the page shows.

### Courses that fit

If institutes have listed courses in your area, they show under [ui: Courses that fit]. To ask about one, select the button that asks that institute about it, check what will be sent, and select [ui: Send inquiry]. Nothing goes to an institute until you send it.

## Your career path

[ui: Career path] brings it together:

- [ui: Target role]: the job you're aiming for. Select [ui: Set it in your skill passport] to set it.
- [ui: Area that suits you]: your career quiz result.
- [ui: Your skills]: your best graded result in each area. Once you have an area, you also see good things to learn next.
- [ui: Next steps]: what to do now.
- [ui: Salary data]: shown only from a named, licensed source. Edulexa doesn't predict salaries or promotions.

## Course suggestions

After a voice defense, [ui: Recommendations] suggests courses for the skills it said to strengthen. Partner courses are sponsored, and the page says so.

To hear from a course provider, select the course's button to share your details, then [ui: Share my details]. They get your name, your e-mail address and the skills to improve, never your scores, transcripts or recordings. Under [ui: Details you've shared], select [ui: Withdraw] at any time: the provider is asked to stop contacting you and delete your details.
