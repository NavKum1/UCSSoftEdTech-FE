Voice vivas and speaking practice use your microphone. There's no separate test page, but you can check it in a minute.

## Before you start

- Find a quiet place. A headset with a microphone helps in a noisy room.
- Close other windows or apps that use the microphone, such as a video call.
- Do the viva in one window or tab only. If it's open somewhere else, the page asks you to end it there first.

## Allow the microphone

When you select [ui: Start the viva by voice], or [ui: Start answering] in speaking practice, your browser asks to use the microphone. Choose to allow it.

If you blocked it before, the page says your browser didn't let it use the microphone. Select the icon next to the site's address in your browser, allow the microphone, and reload the page.

## Check that it hears you

- In [Speaking practice](/prep/delivery), a bar next to [ui: Listening] moves while you talk. If the page says it didn't hear an answer, check that the right microphone is selected in your computer's sound settings.
- In a voice viva, what you say appears on the screen as you go. If your words appear, your microphone works.

## Which browser

Edulexa doesn't list supported browsers. Speaking practice counts your words with the browser's own speech recognition, which Chrome, Edge and Safari have. In other browsers it measures only your pauses and when you started.

## If voice doesn't work

In the viva, select [ui: Answer in writing], or [ui: Voice didn't work? Answer in writing] if that's what the page shows. Your teacher sees that voice failed, so it doesn't count against you. See [Do the viva](/help/the-viva).
