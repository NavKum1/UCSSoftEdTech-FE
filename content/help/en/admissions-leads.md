A lead is a person who asked about your courses on Edulexa, asked you for career guidance, or sent the guidance form on your website. Open [Admissions leads](/institute/dashboard).

## Today's work

The boxes at the top show what needs you today: [ui: New, not contacted], [ui: Replied, need your answer], [ui: Follow-ups overdue], [ui: Follow-ups due today] and [ui: My open leads]. Select a box to see those leads.

Below them are four tabs:

- [ui: Leads]: every lead, as a table or a board.
- [ui: People]: one profile per person, with all their inquiries joined by e-mail address or phone number.
- [ui: Follow-ups]: the follow-ups due for you.
- [ui: Insights]: leads per week, your funnel, and leads by source, course and owner.

## Find leads

Choose [ui: All leads], [ui: My leads] or a quick view such as [ui: Unassigned], [ui: High priority] or [ui: Possible spam]. Search by name, e-mail address, phone, course, source, tag or question. [ui: Filters] narrows the list further, and [ui: Save this view] keeps your choice for next time.

[ui: Table] shows a list you can [ui: Sort]. [ui: Board] shows a column per stage: drag a lead to another stage. To change many leads at once, tick them, then use [ui: Assign to…], [ui: Move to…] or [ui: Add tag]. [ui: Export] downloads the leads shown as a spreadsheet.

## Open a lead

Select a lead to open it. At the top are their contact details and buttons for what you can do:

- [ui: Log call]: record how the call went.
- [ui: Email]: write it yourself, start from a template, or let AI draft it. Send it now or later. Their reply comes back to the lead.
- [ui: WhatsApp]: opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send from your phone.
- [ui: SMS]: sends a text message, once your institute has connected SMS.
- [ui: Meeting]: book a time. They get the time and a calendar invite by e-mail.
- [ui: Note]: only your team sees notes.

Every e-mail has a link to stop further contact. If the person uses it, the lead is marked [ui: Do not contact], and e-mail, SMS and WhatsApp are blocked.

Under the buttons, three tabs keep the history: [ui: Communication] (every call, message and note), [ui: Follow-ups] and [ui: What they shared] (their answers and results).

## Stages, owners and follow-ups

Each lead moves through stages: [ui: New], [ui: Contacted], **Counseling booked**, [ui: Applied], [ui: Enrolled] or [ui: Lost]. A lead moves to [ui: Contacted] on its own when you call, e-mail or message them. Select [ui: Mark as lost] to close a lead, and say why.

Choose the lead's [ui: Owner] from your team. Add [ui: Tags], such as "scholarship", and tick [ui: High priority] for the ones to call first.

To plan the next contact, open [ui: Follow-ups] on the lead, write [ui: What needs doing], pick the [ui: Due time] and [ui: Who], and select [ui: Schedule]. That person gets an e-mail reminder when it's due.

## Leads that need a look

- [ui: Possible spam]: kept out of your leads until you select [ui: Not spam].
- [ui: Incomplete]: they started your website form and gave their contact details, but haven't sent it yet.
- [ui: Email not confirmed]: they haven't clicked the link in the confirmation e-mail yet, so the address may not be theirs.
- [ui: Duplicate]: the same e-mail address sent another inquiry. Check before you contact them twice.

> If the page says you're not on your institute's team list, no leads can be yours yet. Ask your admin to add you to the team in Admissions settings, with the e-mail you sign in with.
