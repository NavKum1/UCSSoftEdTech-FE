Schools that signed up online manage their plan on the [ui: Billing] page. If Edulexa's team set your school up on a contract, Billing says so instead: contact us about invoices or changes.

## AI voice minutes

Voice vivas use AI voice minutes. Your plan includes a number each month, and Billing shows how many you've used. When none are left, students answer the viva in writing instead; nothing else stops.

If you need more, use [ui: Buy 100 minutes]. You pay once: by card, or in India by card or UPI. Bought minutes:

- are added as soon as the payment is confirmed;
- are used after your plan's monthly minutes;
- are kept from month to month until your plan ends.

## Change or cancel your plan

- [ui: Change plan] to move between plans, paid monthly or yearly ([ui: Yearly: 2 months free]).
- [ui: Card and invoices] (card payments) opens the page for your card and invoices.
- [ui: Cancel plan]: your school stays open until the end of the trial or the period you've paid for, then becomes read-only. Bought voice minutes end with the plan.

> A read-only school can still sign in, see and export everything until its data is deleted. Choose a plan and [ui: Pay and reopen] to carry on.
