## Decide

Under [ui: Your decision] on the student's evidence page:

1. Score each criterion with a whole number from 0 to 100. Each starts from the AI's suggestion when there is one; change any you disagree with. The weighted score is worked out for you.
2. Choose [ui: Pass], [ui: Resubmit] or [ui: Fail].
3. Write a [ui: Comment for the student]. It's needed to ask for a resubmission or to fail, so the student knows why.
4. Select [ui: Save decision].

The student sees your decision, your scores and your comment. They never see the AI's suggestions.

- **Pass:** if the assessment issues credentials, a signed credential is added to the student's wallet. Anyone can verify it.
- **Resubmit:** the student can hand in again, even after the due date. Their next attempt starts fresh, with your feedback shown to them.
- **Fail:** the student is told they didn't pass, and that they can appeal.

> If the student's work or viva changes after you decide, the evidence page says so.

## Appeals

A student can appeal a decision once, within 14 days. Appeals appear under [ui: Appeals] on the [ui: Review] page. You can't answer an appeal of your own decision: another teacher of the class, or a school admin, does.

Open the appeal, read the student's reason, and write a [ui: Response to the student]. Then either [ui: Keep the decision] or [ui: Change it] and [ui: Save the new decision]. The student sees your response.
