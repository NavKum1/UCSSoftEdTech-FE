Your assessments are under [ui: My assessments]. Open one to see what to do, how you're assessed, and when it's due. When you're ready, select [ui: Start].

[shot: my-assessments]

How you hand in depends on the assessment:

## An essay, report, lab report or presentation

Choose your file (PDF, Word or PowerPoint, up to 25 MB) and select [ui: Upload and hand in]. Or use [ui: Paste the text instead] and [ui: Hand in this text] (50 words at least).

The viva's questions are about what's in your file, so it needs readable text. A scan or a photo of your pages can't be read: save your work as a PDF with text, upload the Word or PowerPoint file, or paste the text. Old formats such as .doc and .ppt need saving as .docx, .pptx or PDF first.

## A project

- A GitHub repository, under [ui: Hand in a GitHub repository]: enter [ui: Your GitHub username] and the [ui: Repository], then select [ui: Check the repository]. To show it's yours, add the code you're given to your GitHub profile bio (or a public gist's description), then select [ui: I've added it, check now]. You can remove the code afterwards.
- A .zip, under [ui: Or upload your project]: your project folder zipped, up to 25 MB, without dependencies or build output such as node_modules.

## A coding task or an exam

Select [ui: Open the coding task] or [ui: Open the exam], do it there, then come back and select [ui: Hand in my solution] or [ui: Hand in my exam].

> You can replace what you handed in until your viva starts. From then on, your work is fixed, because the questions are about it.

Next comes the viva (and, for some coding assessments, a short change task first). See [Do the viva](/help/the-viva).
