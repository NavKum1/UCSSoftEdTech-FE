## The rubric

The [ui: Rubric] is the list of what you'll score, each from 0 to 100. Each template starts with its own criteria; an essay, for example, starts with the argument, the evidence and sources, understanding their own essay, handling other views, and explaining clearly.

- Change any criterion's name, what a strong answer shows, and its weight.
- Use [ui: Add a criterion] for more (between 2 and 8 criteria).
- The weights must add up to 100. The builder shows the running total.

When you decide, you score each criterion, and the weighted score out of 100 is worked out for you.

## The viva

The [ui: Viva] is a short oral examination about the student's own work. An AI examiner asks the questions, one at a time, each about what the student handed in. It never gives hints, answers or scores.

- [ui: How students answer]: [ui: By voice], [ui: In writing], or [ui: The student chooses].
- [ui: Questions]: between 3 and 10.
- [ui: Minutes]: about how long a voice viva lasts, between 5 and 30.
- [ui: Language]: the viva's language, or [ui: Each student's own choice] from their settings.

Students are judged on understanding, never on accent, grammar or spelling.

> Voice vivas use your school's AI voice minutes. When there are none left, or voice doesn't work for a student, they answer in writing instead, and you see that it was written. See [AI voice minutes and billing](/help/billing-and-voice-minutes).

## A change task (code only)

Project and coding assessments can also ask for one small change to the student's own code before the viva. See [Add a change task](/help/change-task).
