## Your results

[ui: My results] lists each coding challenge you've handed in, and any assessment an employer invited you to. Each one shows its tests, your defense and where the review stands. Select one to open its report:

- [ui: Tests passed], your [ui: Defense score], the [ui: Review] and [ui: Integrity].
- Feedback written for you: [ui: Strengths], [ui: Gaps] and [ui: Three next steps].
- [ui: Your defense]: your scores, the skills to work on, and the questions and answers.

A person reviews every result. Nothing is decided automatically.

Your written exam results are on the [ui: Written exams] page, under [ui: My attempts and credentials].

### Appeal a result

If something went wrong, or you think the result was unfair, appeal it from the report.

1. Under [ui: What is the appeal about?], choose what happened: [ui: Technical problem] (something broke, or you lost work), [ui: Accessibility], [ui: Integrity review] (you think your work is being questioned unfairly), [ui: Scoring error] or [ui: Other].
2. Under [ui: Explain what happened], say what happened, when, and what you'd like looked at again (at least 50 characters).
3. Select [ui: Submit appeal].

A reviewer who wasn't part of the first review looks at it, and you get their answer by e-mail. You can [ui: Withdraw appeal] while it's open.

### Ask for an accommodation

Under [ui: Accommodations], choose the [ui: Type] of help you need: [ui: Extra time], [ui: Written defense instead of voice], [ui: Screen-reader support] or [ui: Other]. Tick [ui: Only for this challenge (otherwise for all my assessments)] if it's just for this one. Under [ui: What do you need?], describe what would help (you don't have to share a diagnosis), and select [ui: Send request]. You get an e-mail when a reviewer decides.

## Your credentials

A credential is a signed record of something you passed. Signed means Edulexa vouches for it: anyone can check it's real and unchanged. Yours are all in [ui: Credentials].

To share one:

1. Select [ui: Share].
2. Say who it's for (only you see this), and tick what to show with your results: [ui: Submitted code], the [ui: Voice defense recording] or the [ui: Voice defense transcript].
3. Under [ui: Link expires after], choose from 1 to 90 days, then select [ui: Create link].
4. Select [ui: Copy link]. The link is shown only once, but you can always create another.

Under each credential, the share links list how often each link was opened. Select [ui: Revoke] next to a link to stop it working.

Other ways to use a credential:

- [ui: Use for a job application]: if an employer invited you to an assessment, answer with this credential instead of taking it again.
- [ui: Download Open Badge]: for badge wallets and other organizations' systems.
- [ui: Download learner record]: a signed file of the credentials you choose, for a registrar or another school.

> Revoking a whole credential can't be undone. All its links stop working, and anyone who checks it sees it was revoked.

## Your skill passport

Your [ui: Skill passport] is your record of skills and credentials, built from your graded results. Each skill shows your best result; [ui: Verified] means a credential backs it.

- Under [ui: How you appear], set the [ui: Name] and [ui: Target role] it shows, then select [ui: Save].
- Switch sharing on so it says [ui: Sharing is on], then select [ui: Copy] to copy its link. Anyone with the link sees your name, target role, skills and credentials, never your e-mail or your activity.
- Shared it too widely? Select [ui: Create a new link (the old one stops working)], or switch sharing off.
