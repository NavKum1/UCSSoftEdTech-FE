Mentors are alumni and professionals your career center has approved. They volunteer, so be clear about what you'd like help with. Open [Mentoring](/student/mentoring).

## Find a mentor

[ui: Suggested for you] lists mentors who fit what you want. Select [ui: Edit what I'm looking for] to choose topics, industries and fields of work, then [ui: Save preferences]. Suggestions also use the major on your [career record](/student/record).

Or search under [ui: Browse mentors]. Each mentor shows what they help with and how many spots are open.

## Ask for mentoring

1. On a mentor's card, select **Ask for mentoring**.
2. Introduce yourself and say why you chose them. Add your goals, one per line (up to 5).
3. Tick the box to agree that your name, email and phone are shared with the mentor if you both accept.
4. Select [ui: Send request].

The request shows under [ui: My mentorships] as [ui: Waiting for mentor]. At some schools the career center confirms each match first: [ui: Waiting for career center]. You can have only a few requests waiting at once. Changed your mind? Select [ui: Withdraw request] before it's accepted.

## Once you're matched

The mentorship becomes [ui: Active], and you both see each other's contact details. Then you can:

- send private messages, here or in [Messages](/help/messages). The career center reads them only if one of you reports a problem.
- [ui: Log a session] after you meet. Notes are private to the two of you; a short summary for the career center is optional.
- [ui: Edit goals].

When you're done, select [ui: Mark completed], or [ui: End early]. Then leave a rating and select [ui: Send feedback]. Only the career center sees it.

> If something feels wrong, select [ui: Report a problem]. You can also block the mentor, which ends the mentorship.

If you're an alum, you can mentor students too: select [ui: Become a mentor] on your [alumni home page](/alumni).
