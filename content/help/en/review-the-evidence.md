Open [ui: Review] to see every attempt waiting for your decision across your classes, oldest first. Work handed in after the due date is marked [ui: Late]. Select [ui: Review] next to a student to see their evidence.

[shot: review-queue]

## What the evidence page shows

- **The work.** Whatever the student handed in: the text of their document by page or slide (with [ui: Download the original]), their repository or project files, their coding task with its tests, or their exam answers.
- **The change task**, if the assessment has one.
- **The viva.** Every question and answer, whether it was by voice or in writing, and how long it took.
- The [ui: Suggested scores]: the AI's suggestion for each criterion, with a short reason and, where it can, a quote from the student's own answers or work. It's only a suggestion: students never see it, and you decide.
- The [ui: Points to talk about]: patterns worth asking the student about, for example work much like another student's, answers read out from the document, or a viva answered in writing after voice failed. They're not findings, and nothing here decides anything.

[shot: suggested-scores]

> Each time you open a student's evidence, it's recorded, as it is for any student record.

When you're ready, decide on the same page. See [Decide and handle appeals](/help/decide-and-appeals).
