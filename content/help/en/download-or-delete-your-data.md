[Privacy and your data](/settings/privacy) is where you download a copy of your data, or delete it. These are your rights under privacy laws.

## Open it

- **If you use Edulexa on your own:** open your account menu (your initials, top right) and select [ui: Privacy and your data].
- **Everyone else:** open your account menu, select [ui: Your account], and under [ui: More settings] select [ui: Privacy and your data].

## Download a copy

Select [ui: Download my data]. You get one file with everything in your account: your profile, your assessments and practice, the work you handed in, interviews, scores and feedback, credentials, appeals and accommodations, and the records schools, employers and the law keep about you. The file's name ends in .json, and it opens in a web browser.

Two things aren't in it: the details of integrity checks, and reviewers' private notes. The file says why, and how to ask for them.

## Delete your data

Download a copy first if you want one. Deleting can't be undone. The page shows what happens to each kind of record:

- [ui: Deleted]: for example what you did in the code editor, interview transcripts, your résumés and your feedback.
- [ui: Your name removed]: your name becomes "Deleted user", your e-mail address is replaced, and credentials you earned are revoked.
- [ui: Kept, without your name]: the code you handed in, test results, defense scores and employers' decisions stay, because employers must be able to show how they decided. They no longer carry your name or e-mail address.
- [ui: Handled by others, or kept by law]: we ask any school that holds records about you to delete them, and the school decides. Records the law requires us to keep stay, without your name, until their time is up.

To go ahead, type DELETE MY DATA in the box, exactly as shown, and select [ui: Delete my data]. The page then shows how many records were deleted, which schools were asked to delete theirs, and what this didn't cover. Then select [ui: Sign out].

> Your sign-in (your e-mail address and password) is kept separately. To delete it too, contact Edulexa support: see [Get help and contact support](/help/help-and-support). Until then, signing in again takes you back to your emptied account.
