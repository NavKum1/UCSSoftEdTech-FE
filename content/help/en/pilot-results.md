The results page brings together every assessment in a period. Teachers find it under [ui: Results] and see their own classes. School admins find it under [ui: Assessment results] and see the whole school.

If your school is in a pilot, the page covers the pilot's dates; otherwise choose the dates you want.

## What it shows

- **Completion:** how many students were in the classes, started, handed in, finished the viva, and have a decision.
- **Review time:** how long teachers spent actively on each student's evidence before deciding. Only time with the page open, in front and in use is counted.
- **The AI and your teachers:** how often the AI's suggested scores were within 5 points of the teacher's final score, how often they were left unchanged, and the average difference.
- **Points to talk about:** how often the evidence raised each kind of point.
- **Appeals:** how many are open, upheld or changed.
- **Students' answers:** after the viva, students can answer two questions (was it fair, and did the viva reflect their own work) and leave a comment. Answers are anonymous: they're kept without the student or the attempt, and shown only once there are at least 3.

Each assessment has its own figures below the totals, and the whole page can be downloaded as a CSV for a review meeting.
