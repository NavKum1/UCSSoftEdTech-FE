When you graduate, your school's career center moves you from its students to its alumni. You get a notification: **Welcome to the alumni network**. If you've graduated and still show as a student, ask your career center to update your record.

## What stays the same

You sign in the same way, with the same e-mail address and password. Nothing in your account is deleted when you graduate. You keep:

- your [résumés](/help/resumes), cover letters and job checks;
- your [credentials and skill passport](/help/credentials-and-passport);
- your results, your career record and your conversations with the career center;
- your privacy choices. See [Your data and privacy](/help/your-data).

## Your alumni home page

When you sign in, your [alumni home page](/alumni) opens. It shows two cards:

- [ui: Mentoring]: help current students as a mentor. See [Be a mentor](/help/be-a-mentor).
- [ui: Career services]: select [ui: Open career services] to open the career pages you used as a student.

To come back to your alumni home page from the career pages, open your account menu (the circle with your initials, top right) and select [ui: My school] under [ui: Switch to].

## What you can still use

Your school's career pages work for you as they did before. You can:

- [book an appointment](/help/appointments) or join a group session;
- [get your résumé or documents reviewed](/help/document-review);
- [message the career center](/help/messages);
- [find jobs and sign up for interviews](/help/campus-jobs-and-interviews);
- [find a mentor](/help/mentoring) for yourself.

Under [ui: Your record], open [ui: My next step] to tell your career center where you're headed: a job, further study or something else. Only you and your career center see it.

> Your school decides who can book each kind of appointment and group session. Some are for students only, and some are for alumni only, so your choices may differ from before.

## Need help?

Your school helps first. The help center shows your school's contact at the end of each article. See [Get help and contact support](/help/help-and-support).
