On the recruiter portal, your company sends assessments to candidates, reviews what they did, and records fair decisions. Edulexa never advances or turns down a candidate on its own. A person on your team always decides.

## Get access

1. Create a free Edulexa account, or sign in.
2. Open [recruiter access](/access/recruiter). Fill in [ui: Company], [ui: Company website] and [ui: Your job title], then select [ui: Send request].
3. Edulexa checks every request before giving anyone access to candidates' information. Requests from a company e-mail address are approved faster. We e-mail you when it's decided.
4. Once it's approved, sign out and sign in again, then select [ui: Open the recruiter portal].

If a page says your recruiter account isn't connected to a company yet, sign out and sign in again. If that doesn't help, contact Edulexa support.

## Find your way around

The menu at the top has four parts, in the order of the work:

- [ui: Candidates]: the assessments you sent, where each candidate is, and your decisions.
- [ui: Review]: [ui: Evidence packs], [ui: Work trials], [ui: Proctored sessions] and [ui: Challenge results].
- [ui: Fairness]: [ui: Appeals], [ui: Accommodations], [ui: Validity & bias audit] and [ui: Transparency].
- [ui: Settings]: [ui: Team] and [ui: ATS connections].

On a phone, select [ui: Menu] to see every page. The bell at the top opens your notifications.

## Your team

Open [ui: Team] under [ui: Settings]. It lists the recruiters at your company, each [ui: Joined] or [ui: Invited]. Everyone on the team sees the same candidates, reviews and settings.

If you manage the team, your name shows [ui: Manages the team], and you can invite colleagues:

1. Under [ui: Invite a colleague], fill in [ui: First name], [ui: Last name] and [ui: Work e-mail].
2. Select [ui: Send invitation]. A new colleague gets an e-mail to set a password. Someone who already has an Edulexa account is added and told by e-mail.
3. If an invitation got lost, select [ui: Resend].

To withdraw an invitation or remove a colleague, select the bin button next to their name. Someone you remove is signed out. Only Edulexa can change the access of the people who manage the team.

## Connect your applicant tracking system

If your company uses an applicant tracking system (ATS), such as Greenhouse, Lever or Workday, [ui: ATS connections] lets you invite candidates from it and get their results back there. Results carry the reviewer's decision, if there is one, and a link to the evidence pack. They never carry an automatic verdict.

Your IT team usually sets this up. Under [ui: New connection], they choose the [ui: Applicant tracking system], give the connection a [ui: Display name] and enter what that system asks for. Then they select [ui: Create connection]. Edulexa shows the keys for your system once, so copy them before you select [ui: I have saved these values]. [ui: Send test] checks that results arrive.
