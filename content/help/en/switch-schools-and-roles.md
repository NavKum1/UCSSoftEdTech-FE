## More than one school

If you belong to more than one school in the same role, for example as a counselor at two colleges, a list at the top of your school's pages shows the school you're working in. Choose the other school, and the page opens again with that school's menu, people and look.

This browser remembers your choice. On another computer or browser, choose again. If the list shows [ui: Choose school…], pick a school before you go on: until you do, some pages can't tell which school you mean.

## More than one role

When you sign in, Edulexa opens the area for your main role. To move to another area, open your account menu (the circle with your initials, top right). Under [ui: Switch to] are the other areas you can work in, such as:

- [ui: Career center], if you're on a career center's staff;
- [ui: Faculty], if you teach;
- [ui: Employer], if you also recruit for a company at a school;
- [ui: My school], if you also study at a school, or graduated from one, on Edulexa;
- [ui: Practice and results], for your own practice;
- [ui: Recruiter portal], if you also recruit;
- [ui: Institute], if you also work at a guidance institute.

[ui: Switch to] shows only when there's another area for you.

### Career center staff who also teach

If you're on a career center's staff and also teach, you start in the career center. To open your classes, select [ui: Faculty] under [ui: Switch to]. To go back, select [ui: Career center]. School admins also find [ui: Class reviews] and [ui: Assessment results] in the career center's menu.

[Your account](/help/your-account) lists every school and role you have.

## A school or role is missing

Your school admin adds people and gives them their roles. Ask them first. See [Add people to your school](/help/add-people).
