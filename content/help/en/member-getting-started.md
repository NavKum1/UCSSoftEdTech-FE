You don't need a school to use Edulexa. On your own, you can practice real work, prove what you can do, and keep the proof in one place.

## Your dashboard

[Dashboard](/dashboard) is your home page. It shows:

- [ui: Get started], and later [ui: Up next]: a few good next steps, such as the career quiz or a skills exam. Select [ui: Start] on one.
- [ui: Current streak]: how many days in a row you've practiced. [ui: Active days] counts the last 30 days.
- [ui: Verified credentials]: the credentials you've earned so far.
- [ui: Interview minutes]: the voice minutes you have left, and when they renew.
- [ui: Skills], [ui: Recent results] and [ui: Credentials]: these fill in as your work is graded.
- [ui: Career track]: your career quiz result, or [ui: Take the quiz].

## The menus

- [ui: Practice]: interview and speaking practice, engineering drills, coding challenges, written exams and the leaderboard.
- [ui: Guidance]: the career quiz, your career path, course suggestions, mentoring, and your questions to Edulexa.
- [ui: Your record]: [ui: My results], [ui: Credentials], [ui: Skill passport], [ui: Résumés], [ui: Job check], [ui: Cover letters] and [ui: Campus] (tasks a school gives you, how campus jobs work, and a connection check; see [Campus](/help/member-campus)).

Point at a menu to see its pages. The pages of the menu you're in also show as a bar under the header. On a phone, open the menu button at the top.

Your name at the top right opens the account menu, with [ui: Your account], [ui: Pricing and minutes] and [ui: Privacy and your data]. The bell opens your notifications.

## Voice minutes

Interview practice and voice defenses are spoken conversations, so they use voice minutes. Your plan gives you minutes each month. They don't roll over. [Pricing and minutes](/pricing) shows your plan, the other plans and top-up minutes, which are used after your monthly minutes.

## Need help?

Use the question-mark button at the top of any page. If a guide doesn't answer your question, e-mail support@edulexa.com.
