[ui: Validity & bias audit] under [ui: Fairness] helps you check two things: whether assessment scores predict how people do in the job, and whether results differ between groups of candidates.

## Record hiring outcomes

1. Under [ui: Record a hiring outcome], enter the [ui: Candidate email].
2. Tick [ui: Hired] if you hired them. When you know them, add their [ui: Manager performance rating], from 1 to 5, and whether they were [ui: Retained after 90 days].
3. Select [ui: Save outcome].

The outcome is linked to the candidate's most recent piece of work.

## Do scores predict outcomes?

[ui: Predictive validity] compares the share of tests passed and the voice defense score with the manager's rating and with staying 90 days. For each pair it shows the number of hires, the correlation (how closely the two go together), its [ui: Likely range (95%)], and an [ui: Interpretation] in plain words. You need at least 30 hires with outcomes for a meaningful figure. To see one coding challenge, choose it in the list.

## Bias audit data

[ui: Bias audit data] covers candidates who chose to describe themselves, by gender, race or ethnicity, age band and disability. For each group it shows:

- [ui: Selection rate]: the share a person advanced.
- [ui: Scoring rate]: the share whose voice defense score is above the middle score of everyone.
- [ui: Impact ratio]: the group's rate divided by the highest group's rate. Below 0.80 is marked, because it fails the four-fifths rule and should be looked into.

Describing themselves is voluntary for candidates, and the data is only reported as combined figures. Groups of fewer than 5 candidates aren't shown, to protect their privacy. Choose a challenge and dates if you like, then select [ui: Download spreadsheet (CSV)] to give the data to your auditor.

> Under New York City's Local Law 144, the bias audit itself must be done by an independent auditor, and its summary published before use. This page gives you the data for it.

## Share your outcomes in the public report

Edulexa's public transparency report shows whether assessment scores predict how people do in the job. Open [ui: Transparency] under [ui: Fairness] and switch on [ui: Include our hire-outcome data] to add yours. It's off until you switch it on.

Your outcomes are combined with those of other employers who take part. Your company is never named, and nothing is published until enough employers and hires take part. You can switch it off at any time. Reports already published don't change.
