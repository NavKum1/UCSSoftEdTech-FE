## Find an internship

Internships offered through your school are on the [Job board](/student/jobs), marked [ui: Internship]. You apply as for any job: see [Campus jobs and interviews](/help/campus-jobs-and-interviews).

## Register it for credit

If your school gives academic credit for internships, open [Internships](/student/experiences):

1. Select [ui: Register an experience].
2. Choose the credit program and the organization (or enter a new one). Add your site supervisor and their work email, your role, your dates, hours per week and what you want to learn.
3. Save, upload any documents the program asks for, then select [ui: Submit for approval].

A faculty member approves it first, then the career center. To change something, select [ui: Edit details].

## Sign the learning agreement

Once it's approved, you, your site supervisor and your faculty member each sign the learning agreement. Type your full name, tick that you agree, and select [ui: Sign agreement]. Your supervisor signs from a link sent by email, without an account. If details in the agreement change later, everyone signs again.

## While you work

From your start date, [ui: Log hours] each week. Your supervisor approves them. Answer the reflection questions and select [ui: Save reflection]. Your faculty member may comment.

## What your school tracks

The page shows each step: [ui: Submitted], approved, [ui: Agreement signed], [ui: Active], [ui: Evaluations] and [ui: Completed]. Your supervisor is asked for a midterm evaluation when you start and a final one after your end date. Your faculty member then records your grade. The page also shows your approved hours against the hours needed, and everything that happened, with dates.

You can [ui: Withdraw] at any step before it's completed.

> On an F-1 visa? The career center can approve only after the CPT dates on your I-20 and your school's approval date for it are recorded.
