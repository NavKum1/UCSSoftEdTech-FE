Open [ui: Your record] and select [ui: Campus].

## Tasks from your school

Some schools and training programs give their students coding tasks on Edulexa. When one adds your e-mail address to a class or group, its tasks show under [ui: Tasks from your school], with the date each one opens and is due.

If no school has added you, you see [ui: No tasks from a school yet]. Nothing is wrong: you don't need a school to practice. Select [ui: Try a coding challenge] or [ui: Practice an interview].

A school's task has three steps:

1. Select [ui: Start] (later [ui: Continue]) and write or fix code in the browser. Your changes are saved as you go.
2. Submit it. Hidden tests run on your code, and your score is the share of tests that pass.
3. Explain your code in a short voice conversation with an AI interviewer. You need a microphone and a quiet place.

Your test and defense scores then show on the task. The school sees them too, with a readiness level: [ui: Ready], [ui: Nearly ready] or [ui: Needs practice].

### Defense language

Once a school has added you, [ui: Defense language] shows on Campus. Choose the language you'd like to explain your code in and select [ui: Save]. You can still change it when the conversation starts.

## Jobs through a school

Employers offer campus jobs, internships and interviews through a school's career center, so only that school's students and graduates can apply. Edulexa doesn't list jobs for people without a school.

- If your school uses Edulexa, sign in with the e-mail address your school has for you. [ui: My school] then shows under [ui: Switch to] in your account menu, and Campus shows [ui: Open My school].
- Applying for a job you found somewhere else? Select [ui: Check your résumé against a job] to see where your résumé meets its requirements. See [Résumés, job check and cover letters](/help/member-resumes).

## Check your connection

Before a voice interview or a defense, select [ui: Check my connection]. It runs 5 quick checks and a small download, and uses very little data. You see one of:

- [ui: Good]: you're ready.
- [ui: Usable, with care]: close other apps and tabs, especially video, and stay close to your Wi-Fi router.
- [ui: Try a better network]: move closer to your router, use a wired connection or find a stronger mobile signal, then check again.

> To check your microphone, see [Check your microphone and browser](/help/microphone-and-browser).
