## Your credentials

When you pass an assessment that issues credentials, a signed credential is added to [Credentials](/credentials). Signed means Edulexa vouches for it: anyone can check that it's real and unchanged. Each shows your scores and until when it's valid; class assessment results don't expire.

To share one:

1. Select [ui: Share].
2. Say who it's for (only you see this), tick the evidence to include, and choose when the link expires, from 1 to 90 days.
3. Select [ui: Create link], then [ui: Copy link]. The link is shown only once, but you can always create another.

Anyone with the link sees the signed results and the evidence you ticked. Under each credential, the share links list shows each link and how often it was opened. Select [ui: Revoke] to stop one working.

> Revoking a whole credential can't be undone: all its links stop working, and anyone who checks it sees it was revoked.

## Your skill passport

Your [Skill passport](/passport) is your record of skills and credentials, built from your graded results. Each skill shows your best result. [ui: Verified] means a credential backs it.

[shot: passport-sharing]

To share it, switch sharing on so it says [ui: Sharing is on], then select [ui: Copy]. Anyone with the link sees your name, target role, skills and credentials, never your email or your activity. Under [ui: How you appear], set the name and target role it shows.

Shared it too widely? Select [ui: Create a new link (the old one stops working)], or switch sharing off.

## How others check

Each credential in your passport has a [ui: Verify] link to its public page, which shows that it's genuine, or that it was revoked.
Your credentials and passport stay in your account after you graduate.
