## Campus drives

A campus drive is a job an employer sent your school. Once it's approved (see [Approve employers and their jobs](/help/employers-and-approvals)), eligible students can apply.

Open a drive from [ui: Campus drives] to:

- Change the deadline under [ui: Change the deadline], then select [ui: Save changes].
- Select [ui: Close applications], or [ui: Open applications] again.
- Follow the selection with the employer: its rounds, the applicants and their results, interview slots and offers.

To add a drive for a company that recruits through your office, use the [ui: New drive] tab. It's approved and open to students straight away.

## On-campus interview schedules

Employers ask for an interview day at your school. Open [ui: Interview schedules] from the [ui: Students] menu.

1. On [ui: Schedules], select [ui: Manage] on a request and choose [ui: Approve] or [ui: Decline], with a note to the employer if you like.
2. Assign a room for each day, then select [ui: Save rooms]. Add your rooms first on the [ui: Rooms] tab.
3. Select [ui: Publish]. Students can then sign up for a time.

On the day, open [ui: Interview day] to see every interview, print the agendas with [ui: Printable agendas], and select [ui: Check in] or [ui: No-show] for each student. If you cancel a student's interview, the next alternate is booked automatically.

## No-shows

Your school can pause sign-ups for students who miss too many interviews. They're listed on [ui: No-shows]; select [ui: Clear] to let a student sign up again. A career admin sets the limit and the cancellation cutoff on [ui: Settings].

## Who does what

Career admins and employer relations staff change drives and run their selection, and they approve, assign rooms to, publish and cancel interview schedules. Every career staff member can see drives and schedules, add a drive, check students in and mark no-shows.

[Interview reports](/career-center/interviews/reports) show slots offered, bookings, attendance and offers by employer.
