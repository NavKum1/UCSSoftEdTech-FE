When you open the career center as a school admin, your home page shows [ui: Get started with Build & Defend]. It lists five steps, and each one ticks itself when your school has really done it. You don't mark anything by hand.

1. [ui: Add your faculty]. Invite your teachers from [ui: People]. Each teacher gets an e-mail with where to sign in. See [Add people to your school](/help/add-people).
2. [ui: Add a class and its students]. Teachers create their classes and import the roster from a spreadsheet. See [Create a class and import its roster](/help/classes-and-rosters).
3. [ui: Create an assessment from a template]. A teacher picks a template, such as an essay or a project defense, and adjusts the rubric and the viva. See [Create an assessment](/help/create-an-assessment).
4. [ui: Give it to a class]. Students are e-mailed and see it under [ui: My assessments]. See [Give an assessment to a class](/help/give-to-a-class).
5. [ui: Decide the first results]. Teachers review each student's work and viva, and decide. See [Review a student's evidence](/help/review-the-evidence).

Each step has a link that takes you to the page where it's done.

> When all five steps are done, the checklist goes away. If your school is in a pilot, your home page then shows the pilot's dates and a link to its results.

## Who does what

- **School admins** invite people, see every class and assessment, and can resolve appeals.
- **Teachers** create classes, assessments and decisions for their own classes.
- **Students** hand in their work and do the viva.
