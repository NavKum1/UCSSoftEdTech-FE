Your guidance forms are the questions people answer when they ask your institute for help choosing an area or a certification. Each answer becomes a lead in your [admissions leads](/institute/dashboard). Open [ui: Guidance forms] under [ui: Forms].

## Make a form

1. Select [ui: New form].
2. Under [ui: Start from], pick a template. Templates are grouped by the kind of school they're for. Use [ui: Show templates for] to see the ones for your country.
3. Give it a [ui: Name] and select [ui: Create and edit]. You can change every question before you publish.

To copy a form you already have, select [ui: Duplicate] on its card.

## Change it

The form opens on [ui: Questions]. Change the text of any question, select [ui: Add question], or [ui: Add a step] to split the form into steps. A preview shows beside it.

The other tabs:

- [ui: Results]: what the form works out from the answers, an outcome, a match score or both, and whether people see their results after they send.
- [ui: Automatic messages]: e-mails, SMS or WhatsApp messages the form sends by itself, to the person or to your team.
- [ui: Settings]: the form's languages, who can fill it in, saving answers for people who don't finish, and checks against fake requests.
- [ui: Translations]: your texts in each language you added.
- [ui: Share]: the link, the QR code and the code for your website.
- [ui: Insights]: visits, how many started and sent the form, and where they came from.

Your changes are saved as you go, as a draft. People keep seeing the published version until you select [ui: Publish]. [ui: Discard changes] goes back to the published form.

## Share it

A new form can't be opened from outside Edulexa yet. In [ui: Share], select [ui: Turn on the public link]. Then:

- copy the link, or select [ui: Download QR (PNG)] for posters, brochures and stalls. Add a [ui: Campaign (optional)] name to see which link a lead came from;
- under [ui: Put it on your website], copy the code into your website, or the code for a button that opens the form. It works on most website builders.

> Your plan may include your own address for the form, such as guidance.yourschool.edu. The person who manages your domain name has to add two records first; the steps are on the page.

## Your forms page

Each form's card shows whether its public link is on, its version, how many people sent it, and [ui: Unpublished changes] if you haven't published your latest changes. One form is [ui: Used for “Ask an advisor”]: Edulexa members who are signed in use it when they ask you for guidance. To change which one, select [ui: Use for “Ask an advisor”] on another form.

At the top of the page, [ui: Statistics] compares your forms, and [ui: Notifications] lists every automatic message sent.

Below your forms, fill in [ui: Your institute's card] and select [ui: Save card]. It's shown when people on Edulexa choose an institute to talk to, with your real courses. Only what you fill in is shown.
