The sign-in page opens when you select [ui: Sign in], or by itself when a page needs you to be signed in.

## Sign in

1. Enter your e-mail address and select **Continue**.
2. Enter your password and select **Sign In**.

On your own computer, tick **Remember me** to stay signed in longer. Leave it off on a shared computer.

Students sign in with the e-mail address their school put on the class list. If you were invited, use the e-mail address the invitation was sent to.

## If your school or company has its own sign-in

Enter your school or work e-mail. If your school or company has connected its own sign-in, you're taken there, and then back to Edulexa.

## If you forgot your password

On the password step, select **Forgot Password?**, enter your e-mail and select **Send reset link**. The link in the e-mail works for 15 minutes. Your new password needs at least 12 characters, can't be your e-mail, and can't be one of your last five.

## If you still can't sign in

- After 10 wrong passwords, sign-in is paused for a minute, and longer if it happens again (up to 15 minutes). Wait, then try again.
- If the page says it has expired, select **Start again**.
- Staff accounts also need a code from an authenticator app. If you see [ui: Two-step sign-in is needed for your role], select [ui: Sign in again] and set it up. It takes about a minute.
- Still stuck? See [Get help and contact support](/help/help-and-support).

## Sign out

Open your account menu (the circle with your initials, top right) and select [ui: Sign out]. This also signs you out of the other apps that use the same account in this browser.
